Storage Instructions
Store the covered pots at 5°C or below and consume within 2 days. The raspberries will gradually soften and release additional juice during storage, so the best texture is obtained within 24 hours. Do not leave the skyr-based pots unrefrigerated for more than 2 hours.

Restaurant Quality Features

Unsweetened cocoa is balanced with a deliberately small amount of honey, vanilla and salt so the chocolate flavour remains dark rather than cloying.
Raspberries are used in both crushed and whole form to create two distinct fruit textures.
The raspberry layer remains visibly marbled through the cocoa skyr rather than being completely homogenised.
Ground flaxseed is incorporated into the dairy base and allowed a short hydration period so it contributes body without remaining dry or powdery.
A small measured quantity of salt intensifies cocoa flavour and moderates perceived bitterness without making the snack taste salty.
The use of high-protein skyr provides substantial protein and creaminess while keeping the snack close to the 200 kcal evening-snack target.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete evening snack including skyr, raspberries, cocoa, flaxseed, honey, vanilla and salt.
This recipe is designed around the approximately 200 kcal evening-snack target.
The general 30 g protein requirement does not apply to evening snacks under the specified meal-plan rules; approximately 19 g protein per serving is therefore compliant.
Skyr provides the majority of the protein while ground flaxseed contributes fibre and predominantly unsaturated fat.
Raspberries and cocoa contribute additional fibre and polyphenol-rich plant ingredients.
Nutrition values are estimates and will vary with the exact skyr and cocoa products used.

Critical Success Factors

Use unsweetened cocoa powder rather than drinking chocolate so sweetness remains controlled.
Mix the cocoa completely into the skyr before adding flaxseed to avoid persistent dry cocoa pockets.
Do not over-process the raspberries; retaining small fruit pieces gives the finished pots better textural contrast.
Allow at least 10 minutes of refrigerated resting after adding ground flaxseed so the texture becomes cohesive.
Keep the reserved surface raspberries as dry as practical to prevent unnecessary dilution of the cocoa-skyr layer.
Store continuously refrigerated because the recipe contains fresh cultured dairy.
