Cocoa, Raspberry & Skyr Pots with Ground Flaxseed

Creamy cocoa-vanilla skyr layered with lightly crushed raspberries and ground flaxseed for a sharp-sweet, chocolatey evening snack with contrasting smooth, juicy and gently nutty textures.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Inspect 140 g raspberries and discard any mouldy, leaking or damaged berries. If washing is necessary, rinse gently under cold running water and dry thoroughly on clean kitchen paper without crushing. Weigh 100 g into the small mixing bowl and reserve the remaining 40 g whole for final assembly.

Ready when: 100 g clean raspberries are allocated to the crushed fruit layer and 40 g intact raspberries are reserved for finishing.

Prep 2

Weigh 300 g plain skyr, 10 g cocoa powder, 14 g ground flaxseed and 8 g honey. Measure 1 tsp vanilla extract and 0.0625 tsp fine salt. Break apart or sift any compacted cocoa powder before mixing.

Ready when: All non-fruit ingredients are accurately measured and ready for immediate assembly.

Method

Step 1

Crush the raspberries

Raspberry layer
Time: 1–2 min

Place the allocated 100 g raspberries in the small bowl. Using the back of a spoon, press and stir until approximately two-thirds of the berries have broken down and released juice while small pieces of fruit remain identifiable.

Ready when: The raspberries form a thick, juicy spoonable mixture with visible pieces rather than a completely smooth puree.
Corrective actions
  • If: The berries remain almost completely whole.
    Do: Continue pressing firmly against the side of the bowl in 15-second intervals until sufficient juice is released.
  • If: The mixture becomes completely smooth.
    Do: Stop processing immediately; the flavour remains suitable but the intended fruit-piece texture will be reduced.
Step 2

Mix the cocoa-skyr base

Cocoa skyr
Time: 2–3 min

Place 300 g skyr in the mixing bowl. Add 10 g cocoa powder, 8 g honey, 1 tsp vanilla extract and 0.0625 tsp fine salt. Stir slowly at first to incorporate the cocoa without dispersing dry powder into the air, then stir more vigorously until the colour is uniform and no visible cocoa or honey streaks remain.

Ready when: The mixture is smooth, glossy and uniformly chocolate-brown with no dry cocoa pockets or unmixed skyr.
Corrective actions
  • If: Dry cocoa remains around the sides or base of the bowl.
    Do: Scrape the bowl thoroughly and continue mixing until all cocoa is fully dispersed.
  • If: The mixture appears unusually stiff.
    Do: Continue stirring for 20-30 seconds before making any adjustment; skyr normally loosens slightly with vigorous mixing.
Step 3

Incorporate the ground flaxseed

Cocoa skyr
Time: 1–2 min

Add the measured 14 g ground flaxseed to the cocoa-skyr mixture. Fold and stir until the flaxseed is evenly dispersed and no concentrated dry patches remain.

Ready when: The flaxseed is evenly distributed throughout the cocoa skyr and the mixture remains thick but spoonable.
Corrective actions
  • If: Ground flaxseed forms dry clumps.
    Do: Press the clumps against the side of the bowl with the spoon and continue folding until fully dispersed.
Step 4

Layer the snack pots

Assembly
Time: 3–4 min

Divide approximately half of the cocoa-skyr mixture between the 2 snack pots. Divide the crushed raspberry mixture equally over the cocoa layer. Add the remaining cocoa-skyr mixture equally over the raspberries. Use the tip of a spoon to make one or two shallow folds through each pot so visible raspberry streaks remain rather than fully mixing the layers.

Ready when: Both pots contain approximately equal portions with distinct cocoa-skyr and raspberry marbling rather than a uniformly mixed pink-brown colour.
Corrective actions
  • If: One pot is visibly heavier than the other.
    Do: Use the kitchen scales to transfer small amounts between pots until the filled weights are approximately equal.
  • If: The raspberry layer becomes completely mixed into the cocoa skyr.
    Do: Stop stirring; flavour remains correct even though visual contrast will be reduced.
Step 5

Add the reserved raspberries

Finishing
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the reserved 40 g whole raspberries equally between the 2 pots, approximately 20 g per serving. Place the berries on the surface without pressing them into the cocoa-skyr mixture.

Ready when: Each pot is topped with an approximately equal quantity of intact fresh raspberries.
Corrective actions
  • If: A reserved raspberry collapses or releases excessive juice.
    Do: Place it into the raspberry-marbling layer rather than using it as a surface garnish.
Step 6

Allow the flaxseed to hydrate

Chilling
Heat source: RefrigeratorTemperature: 5°CTime: 10 min minimum

Fit the lids and refrigerate the assembled pots at 5°C or below for at least 10 minutes before serving. This brief rest allows the ground flaxseed to hydrate slightly and the cocoa mixture to become more cohesive.

Ready when: The cocoa-skyr mixture is chilled, creamy and cohesive, with the raspberry marbling still visible and no dry flaxseed remaining.
Corrective actions
  • If: The mixture appears noticeably thicker than desired after chilling.
    Do: Stir each pot once or twice immediately before eating; the raspberry juices will loosen the texture naturally without requiring additional ingredients.
Serving

Serve one chilled pot per person. Eat through the layers rather than fully stirring before the first spoonful so each serving retains contrast between dark cocoa skyr, tart raspberry ripple, fresh whole berries and the subtle nutty texture of ground flaxseed.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Store the covered pots at 5°C or below and consume within 2 days. The raspberries will gradually soften and release additional juice during storage, so the best texture is obtained within 24 hours. Do not leave the skyr-based pots unrefrigerated for more than 2 hours.
