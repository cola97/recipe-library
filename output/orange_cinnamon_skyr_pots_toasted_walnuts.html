Orange & Cinnamon Skyr Pots with Toasted Walnuts

Recipe info

Creamy cinnamon skyr layered with fresh orange segments and finished with warm, deeply toasted walnuts for a simple evening snack with bright citrus, aromatic spice and crisp texture.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Finely grate the coloured zest from approximately half of 1 washed orange, avoiding the bitter white pith. Peel both oranges, separate them into segments and remove any loose strands of white pith.

Ready when: The orange segments are intact and juicy, and the zest is fine and free from visible white pith.

Prep 2

Weigh 20 g walnuts and roughly chop them into pieces approximately 5-8 mm across.

Ready when: The walnuts remain coarse enough to provide distinct crunch.

Prep 3

Measure 240 g skyr, 1 tsp runny honey and 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon.

Ready when: The skyr, honey and cinnamon are measured and ready for assembly.

Method

Step 1

Toast the walnuts

Walnuts
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: mediumTime: 3–4 min

Place the dry frying pan over medium heat. Add the chopped walnuts and toast for approximately 3-4 minutes, shaking or stirring every 20-30 seconds so all sides heat evenly.

Ready when: The walnuts smell strongly nutty, have deepened slightly in colour and are hot throughout without any blackened or bitter-tasting pieces.
Corrective actions
  • If: The walnuts begin darkening rapidly or smoking.
    Do: Immediately remove the pan from the heat and transfer the walnuts to a cool plate.
  • If: The walnuts remain pale and barely aromatic after 4 minutes.
    Do: Continue toasting for up to 1 additional minute while stirring continuously.
Step 2

Make the orange-cinnamon skyr

Skyr
Time: 0.5–1 min

Combine the 240 g skyr with 1 tsp runny honey, 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon and the prepared orange zest. Stir for approximately 30 seconds until the cinnamon and zest are evenly distributed.

Ready when: The skyr is smooth, evenly cinnamon-speckled and clearly fragrant with fresh orange zest.
Corrective actions
  • If: The cinnamon forms visible dry clumps.
    Do: Press the clumps against the side of the bowl and continue stirring until evenly dispersed.
Step 3

Cool the toasted walnuts

Walnuts
Time: 3 min

Transfer the toasted walnuts out of the hot pan and leave them uncovered for approximately 3 minutes so residual steam and heat can escape.

Ready when: The walnuts are warm or room temperature rather than hot and remain dry and crisp.
Step 4

Build the skyr pots

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Divide the orange-cinnamon skyr evenly between the two serving pots. Arrange the segments from 1 orange over each serving. If eating immediately, scatter half of the toasted walnuts over each pot. If preparing ahead, keep the walnuts in separate dry containers and add them only at the time of eating.

Ready when: Each serving contains an even portion of cinnamon-orange skyr, the segments from approximately 1 orange and half of the toasted walnuts, with the walnuts remaining crisp.
Corrective actions
  • If: The orange segments release substantial juice during assembly.
    Do: Leave excess free juice behind rather than pouring it into the skyr pot.
Serving

Serve the pots chilled or cool, with the toasted walnuts added immediately before eating. Take each spoonful with both orange and cinnamon skyr so the bright citrus contrasts with the creamy dairy and toasted nut flavour.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Refrigerate the skyr and orange pots in sealed containers for up to 2 days. Store the toasted walnuts separately in a dry sealed container and add them only immediately before serving so they remain crisp. Do not freeze the assembled pots because the orange and skyr textures will deteriorate.
