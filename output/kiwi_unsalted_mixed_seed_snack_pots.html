Kiwi & Unsalted Mixed Seed Snack Pots

Recipe info

A simple portable snack of ripe kiwi with a crunchy unsalted mixed-seed topping, packed separately until eating so the seeds stay crisp and the fruit remains fresh.

Equipment
  • Chef's knife
  • Chopping board
  • Kitchen scales
  • 2 divided lidded snack pots
Mise en place

Prep 1

Peel the 2 kiwi fruit, trim away the hard stem ends, then cut the flesh into bite-sized pieces approximately 15-20 mm across.

Ready when: The kiwi pieces are evenly sized, intact and not excessively crushed or watery.

Prep 2

Weigh 50 g unsalted mixed seeds.

Ready when: The seeds are accurately measured and remain completely dry.

Method

Step 1

Portion the kiwi

Assembly
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the prepared kiwi evenly between the two snack pots, giving approximately 1 kiwi per serving. Keep any released fruit juice with the kiwi rather than allowing it into the dry seed compartment.

Ready when: Each pot contains an even portion of fresh kiwi with no fruit or juice entering the dry seed compartment.
Corrective actions
  • If: The kiwi releases a large amount of juice during cutting.
    Do: Transfer the fruit with a slotted spoon or leave excess juice on the chopping board so the snack pot does not become unnecessarily wet.
Step 2

Pack the mixed seeds

Assembly
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the 50 g mixed seeds evenly between the two dry compartments or separate small containers, giving approximately 25 g per serving. Seal both portions so the seeds remain isolated from the kiwi until eating.

Ready when: Each serving contains approximately 25 g completely dry mixed seeds stored separately from the fruit.
Corrective actions
  • If: The snack pots do not have separate dry compartments.
    Do: Carry the seeds in two separate small reusable containers and add them immediately before eating.
Serving

Immediately before eating, scatter the mixed seeds over the kiwi or eat them alongside it. Add the seeds only at the last moment so their crunch contrasts with the juicy fruit.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 1 days

Refrigerate the cut kiwi promptly and use within 24 hours for the best texture. Keep the mixed seeds completely dry and separate until eating. For transport to work or on a car journey, carry the kiwi in an insulated bag with an ice pack and keep chilled until required.
