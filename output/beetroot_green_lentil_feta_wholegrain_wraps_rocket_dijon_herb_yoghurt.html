Beetroot, Green Lentil & Feta Wholegrain Wraps with Rocket and Dijon-Herb Yoghurt

Recipe info

Portable wholegrain wraps filled with earthy beetroot, seasoned green lentils, salty feta and peppery rocket, served with a thick Dijon-parsley yoghurt for a bright, creamy finish without making the wraps soggy.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Drain the green lentils into the colander, rinse thoroughly under cold running water, then shake firmly and leave them to drain while preparing the other ingredients.

Ready when: The lentils are cool, clean-tasting and sufficiently dry that no water collects beneath the colander.

Prep 2

Pat the cooked beetroot thoroughly dry, then cut it into approximately 8 mm dice. Crumble the feta into pieces approximately 5-10 mm across. Check that the rocket is completely dry.

Ready when: The beetroot is evenly diced without excessive surface liquid, the feta is coarsely crumbled and the rocket is dry.

Prep 3

Finely chop the parsley leaves and tender stems.

Ready when: The parsley is finely and evenly chopped without being crushed into a paste.

Method

Step 1

Make the Dijon-herb yoghurt

Sauce
Time: 1–2 min

Combine the 240 g Greek yoghurt with 2 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tbsp lemon juice, approximately two-thirds of the chopped parsley and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Stir until completely even.

Ready when: The sauce is thick, smooth and spoonable, with clear mustard acidity and fresh parsley flavour.
Corrective actions
  • If: The sauce seems excessively thick for dipping.
    Do: Stir in 1 tsp cold water; add no more unless required.
Step 2

Season the lentils and beetroot

Filling
Time: 2–3 min

Place the well-drained lentils and diced beetroot in the mixing bowl. Add the remaining 1 tbsp lemon juice, remaining chopped parsley, 1/8 tsp fine salt and remaining 1/4 tsp black pepper. Fold gently until evenly seasoned, then fold through the crumbled feta, keeping distinct pieces of cheese visible.

Ready when: The filling tastes bright, savoury and properly seasoned, with no watery liquid pooling in the bottom of the bowl and distinct pieces of beetroot and feta remaining.
Corrective actions
  • If: Liquid accumulates at the bottom of the bowl.
    Do: Transfer the mixture briefly to the colander and allow the excess liquid to drain before filling the wraps.
Step 3

Layer the wraps

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Lay the 2 wholemeal wraps flat. Divide the 40 g rocket between them, arranging it in a strip across the central third of each wrap. Divide the beetroot, lentil and feta mixture evenly over the rocket, leaving approximately 40 mm clear at the left and right edges.

Ready when: Each wrap contains an even portion of filling positioned centrally, with the dry rocket forming a barrier between the moist filling and the tortilla.
Corrective actions
  • If: The filling is piled too high for the wrap to close comfortably.
    Do: Spread it into a longer, narrower strip rather than compressing it into a mound.
Step 4

Roll the wraps tightly

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Fold the left and right edges of each tortilla inward over the filling. Bring the lower edge firmly over the filling, compress gently, then continue rolling away from you into a tight cylinder with the seam underneath.

Ready when: Each wrap is tightly enclosed with both ends folded in, no loose filling escaping and the seam secured underneath.
Corrective actions
  • If: The tortilla begins to split while rolling.
    Do: Reduce the compression force and leave the wrap whole rather than attempting to tighten it further.
Step 5

Pack the yoghurt separately

Packing
Time: 1–2 min

Place 1 wrap into each lunch container. Divide the Dijon-herb yoghurt evenly between 2 sealed small sauce pots, giving approximately 120 g per serving. Keep the sauce separate until eating.

Ready when: Each lunch contains 1 sealed wrap and 1 separate portion of thick Dijon-herb yoghurt with no sauce contacting the tortilla during storage.
Serving

Serve each wholegrain wrap with its portion of Dijon-herb yoghurt. Dip each bite into the yoghurt or spoon small amounts into the open end as the wrap is eaten, keeping the remaining tortilla dry and structured.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 1 days

Refrigerate the assembled wraps and Dijon-herb yoghurt promptly and use within 24 hours for the best texture. Keep the yoghurt in separate sealed sauce pots. For transport to work or on a car journey, keep the lunch chilled in an insulated bag with an ice pack and refrigerate on arrival where possible. The seasoned lentil-beetroot filling and yoghurt sauce may separately be refrigerated for up to 2 days before final assembly.
