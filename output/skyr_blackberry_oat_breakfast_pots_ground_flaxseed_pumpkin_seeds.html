Skyr, Blackberry & Oat Breakfast Pots with Ground Flaxseed and Pumpkin Seeds

Recipe info

Make-ahead breakfast pots of thick skyr and jumbo oats with juicy blackberries, ground flaxseed and pumpkin seeds, lightly flavoured with cinnamon and vanilla for a creamy, high-protein breakfast with fresh fruit and contrasting seed crunch.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Inspect the blackberries and discard any damaged or mouldy berries. Rinse gently only if required, then dry thoroughly so excess water does not loosen the breakfast pots.

Ready when: The blackberries are intact, clean and dry on the surface.

Prep 2

Measure 500 g skyr, 70 g jumbo oats, 1 tbsp ground flaxseed, 1 tsp runny honey, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon and 80 ml cold water.

Ready when: All ingredients for the overnight oat base are accurately measured.

Method

Step 1

Mix the skyr and oat base

Oat base
Time: 1–2 min

Combine the skyr, jumbo oats, ground flaxseed, honey, vanilla extract, cinnamon and 80 ml cold water. Stir thoroughly for approximately 45-60 seconds, scraping the base and sides of the bowl so the oats and flaxseed are evenly distributed.

Ready when: The mixture is homogeneous, thick but spoonable, with no dry pockets of oats or clumps of flaxseed.
Corrective actions
  • If: The mixture is too stiff to stir evenly.
    Do: Add 1 tbsp cold water and mix again; add a second tablespoon only if still necessary.
Step 2

Layer the breakfast pots

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Divide approximately half of the skyr-oat mixture between the two pots. Divide approximately half of the blackberries over that layer, then add the remaining oat mixture and finish with the remaining blackberries. Handle the fruit gently so most berries remain whole.

Ready when: Each pot contains an even portion of the skyr-oat mixture and approximately 75 g blackberries, with visible intact fruit and no large pockets of dry oats.
Corrective actions
  • If: Very ripe blackberries release substantial juice during assembly.
    Do: Place the softest berries on the top layer rather than burying them in the oat mixture.
Step 3

Refrigerate overnight

Hydration
Time: 480 min minimum

Seal the pots and refrigerate for at least 8 hours before serving.

Ready when: The jumbo oats are hydrated and pleasantly chewy rather than dry, and the mixture has thickened while remaining creamy and spoonable.
Corrective actions
  • If: The mixture is unusually thick after overnight refrigeration.
    Do: Stir 1 tbsp cold water into each pot immediately before serving.
Step 4

Add the pumpkin seeds

Finishing
Time: 0.5–1 min

Immediately before eating, divide the 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds evenly between the two pots, scattering approximately 1/2 tbsp over each serving.

Ready when: Each breakfast pot has a fresh, dry pumpkin-seed topping that remains crisp against the creamy oats.
Serving

Serve chilled, stirring lightly as you eat so some blackberries break into the cinnamon-vanilla skyr while the remaining berries and pumpkin seeds retain distinct juicy and crunchy textures.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Keep the assembled skyr, oat and blackberry pots sealed and refrigerated for up to 2 days. Store the pumpkin seeds separately and add immediately before eating for the best crunch. For transport, keep the pots chilled in an insulated bag with an ice pack and refrigerate on arrival where possible.
