Description
Savoury high-protein breakfast muffins with deeply browned mushrooms, wilted spinach, cottage cheese and seasoned egg, served with crisp seeded wholemeal toast. The vegetables are cooked dry before assembly so the muffins remain tender and structured rather than watery.

Storage

Refrigerator Days
3
Freezer Months

Storage Instructions
Cool leftover egg muffins promptly, transfer to a sealed container and refrigerate at 5°C or below for up to 3 days. Freeze fully cooled muffins in a sealed freezer-safe container for up to 1 month. Store bread separately and toast fresh whenever practical. Reheat egg muffins only once and ensure they are steaming hot through the centre before eating.

Reheating Methods

Air fryer: reheat refrigerated egg muffins at 160°C for approximately 5-7 minutes, until steaming hot through the centre without excessive surface browning.
Oven: reheat refrigerated egg muffins at 170°C for approximately 8-12 minutes, until steaming hot throughout.
From frozen: thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating using the air fryer or oven method.
Toast bread separately in the toaster immediately before serving.

Restaurant Quality Features

Mushrooms are browned until their released moisture has evaporated, concentrating savoury flavour instead of introducing water into the egg muffins.
Spinach is wilted separately within the mushroom mixture and cooked until the pan is dry, protecting the finished muffins from a wet or rubbery interior.
Whole eggs are combined with additional egg whites and cottage cheese to provide a tender, protein-rich structure without excessive calories.
Dijon mustard supplies restrained acidity and savoury depth, while nutmeg reinforces the spinach and dairy flavours.
Fresh chives and spring onions provide two layers of mild allium character without overwhelming the breakfast.
The muffins are rested briefly after baking so they release cleanly and retain moisture.
Seeded wholemeal toast is prepared immediately before serving to provide crisp contrast against the soft egg muffins.

The recipe is designed around the approximately 400 kcal breakfast target.

Critical Success Factors

Cook the mushrooms until genuinely browned and free from visible moisture before adding spinach.
Do not add hot, steaming vegetables directly to the egg mixture; cool them sufficiently to avoid prematurely coagulating the eggs.
Ensure the spinach mixture is dry before muffin assembly because retained vegetable water will compromise texture.
Distribute vegetable solids evenly among all 6 muffin wells rather than allowing them to settle in the mixing bowl.
Bake until the centre is fully set but avoid prolonged baking after that point, which will make the eggs rubbery.
Rest the baked muffins before removal so the protein structure can firm and release cleanly.
Toast the bread as the muffins finish resting so both components reach the table at their intended textures.
