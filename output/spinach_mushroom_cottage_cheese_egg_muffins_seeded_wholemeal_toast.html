Spinach, Mushroom & Cottage Cheese Egg Muffins with Seeded Wholemeal Toast

Savoury high-protein breakfast muffins with deeply browned mushrooms, wilted spinach, cottage cheese and seasoned egg, served with crisp seeded wholemeal toast. The vegetables are cooked dry before assembly so the muffins remain tender and structured rather than watery.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Inspect 180 g chestnut mushrooms. Wipe or brush away visible soil; avoid soaking them in water. Trim only dry or damaged stem ends, then dice the mushrooms into pieces approximately 8 mm across.

Ready when: The mushrooms are clean, externally dry and evenly diced into approximately 8 mm pieces.

Prep 2

Inspect 120 g baby spinach. Wash only if required by the packaging instructions, then dry thoroughly in a salad spinner or using clean kitchen paper. Remove any visibly damaged leaves or coarse stems.

Ready when: The spinach is clean and as dry as practical before cooking.

Prep 3

Wash the 2 spring onions and the chives if required. Dry thoroughly. Trim the spring-onion roots and damaged green ends, then slice crosswise into approximately 3 mm pieces. Slice the chives crosswise into approximately 2 mm pieces.

Ready when: Spring onions and chives are clean, dry and uniformly sliced.

Prep 4

Crack each of the 3 eggs individually into the small bowl, inspect for shell fragments, then transfer to the large mixing bowl. Weigh 180 g liquid egg whites and 180 g low-fat cottage cheese. Measure 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 0.125 tsp ground nutmeg, 0.25 tsp initial fine salt, 0.125 tsp reserved fine salt and 0.25 tsp black pepper.

Ready when: All egg-mixture ingredients are accurately measured and ready for assembly.

Prep 5

Measure 6 g olive oil. Allocate approximately 4 g for cooking the vegetables and approximately 2 g for greasing the muffin tin. Weigh 90 g seeded wholemeal bread and keep it covered until the toasting stage.

Ready when: Cooking oil is divided by function and the bread is ready for later toasting.

Method

Step 1

Preheat the oven and grease the muffin tin

Oven preparation
Heat source: OvenSetting: 180°C fanTemperature: 180°CTime: 5–10 min

Preheat a fan oven to 180°C. Apply approximately 2 g of the measured olive oil as a very thin film across the base and lower sides of all 6 muffin-tin wells.

Ready when: The oven has reached 180°C and all 6 muffin wells have a continuous but minimal oil coating without visible pools.
Corrective actions
  • If: Oil pools visibly at the bottom of a muffin well.
    Do: Redistribute the excess oil across the remaining wells using clean kitchen paper or a pastry brush.
Step 2

Brown the mushrooms

Vegetable filling
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: Medium-highTime: 6–9 min

Place the large frying pan over medium-high gas heat for approximately 1 minute. Add the remaining approximately 4 g olive oil, followed immediately by the 180 g diced mushrooms. Spread into a single layer where practical. Cook while stirring or tossing approximately every 45-60 seconds. After the mushrooms begin releasing moisture, add approximately 0.0625 tsp of the measured initial salt.

Ready when: The mushrooms have released and evaporated their visible moisture, are reduced in volume and show distinct golden-brown surfaces rather than appearing grey and wet.
Corrective actions
  • If: Liquid accumulates heavily in the pan and the mushrooms steam without colouring.
    Do: Increase the heat and spread the mushrooms into a thinner layer; continue until all free liquid evaporates before proceeding.
  • If: The mushrooms blacken before releasing their moisture.
    Do: Reduce the heat to medium and stir immediately.
Step 3

Wilt and dry the spinach

Vegetable filling
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: MediumTime: 2–4 min

Reduce the heat to medium. Add the prepared 120 g spinach and sliced spring onions to the browned mushrooms. Turn continuously until the spinach collapses. Continue cooking uncovered while stirring until liquid released by the spinach has completely evaporated.

Ready when: The spinach is fully wilted, the spring onions are softened but still green and no visible liquid remains in the pan when the vegetables are moved aside.
Corrective actions
  • If: Visible green liquid remains in the pan after the spinach has wilted.
    Do: Increase the heat slightly and continue cooking uncovered while stirring until the pan surface is dry.
  • If: The vegetables begin catching before moisture has evaporated.
    Do: Reduce the heat slightly and keep the mixture moving continuously.
Step 4

Cool the vegetable mixture

Vegetable filling
Time: 4–6 min

Remove the frying pan from the heat and spread the cooked vegetables into a thin layer across the pan surface. Leave uncovered until strong visible steam has stopped and the mixture is warm rather than piping hot.

Ready when: The vegetable mixture is warm but no longer releasing substantial steam and remains free from pooled liquid.
Corrective actions
  • If: The vegetables remain very hot after 6 minutes.
    Do: Transfer them to a cool plate and spread into a thinner layer for an additional 2-3 minutes.
Step 5

Combine the egg and cottage-cheese base

Egg mixture
Time: 1–2 min

To the 3 cracked eggs in the large mixing bowl add 180 g liquid egg whites, 180 g cottage cheese, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, the prepared 10 g chives, 0.125 tsp nutmeg, the remaining approximately 0.1875 tsp of the initial salt and 0.25 tsp black pepper. Whisk or stir vigorously until the eggs and whites are homogeneous while small cottage-cheese curds remain visible.

Ready when: The mixture is uniformly seasoned and fluid, with cottage-cheese curds evenly suspended rather than collected in one area.
Corrective actions
  • If: Large cottage-cheese clumps remain concentrated in one area.
    Do: Break them apart gently with the spatula and continue mixing until evenly distributed.
Step 6

Combine filling and portion the muffin mixture

Assembly
Time: 3–5 min

Add the cooled mushroom-spinach mixture to the egg mixture and fold until evenly distributed. Divide the complete mixture equally among all 6 greased muffin wells. Each well should contain a similar proportion of vegetables, cottage-cheese curds and liquid egg mixture and should remain below the top edge.

Ready when: All 6 wells contain approximately equal quantities and no well is filled sufficiently high to overflow during baking.
Corrective actions
  • If: Vegetables settle at the bottom of the mixing bowl during portioning.
    Do: Stir the bowl gently between every 1-2 filled wells to redistribute the solids.
  • If: One well is visibly more full than another.
    Do: Transfer small spoonfuls between wells until the levels are approximately equal.
Step 7

Bake the egg muffins

Baking
Heat source: OvenSetting: 180°C fanTemperature: 180°CTime: 16–21 min

Place the muffin tin on the centre rack of the preheated 180°C fan oven. Bake without opening the door during the first 14 minutes. Thereafter assess visually through the oven door where possible.

Ready when: The muffins are risen, lightly golden at the exposed edges and centres appear set rather than liquid. A clean knife inserted into the centre of the largest muffin emerges without raw liquid egg; moist cottage-cheese residue is acceptable.
Corrective actions
  • If: The muffin tops brown strongly while the centres remain visibly wet.
    Do: Reduce the oven to 170°C and continue baking in 2-minute intervals until the centre passes the stated endpoint.
  • If: The centres remain visibly liquid after 21 minutes.
    Do: Continue baking in 2-minute intervals and reassess after each interval.
Step 8

Rest and release the muffins

Resting
Time: 4–6 min

Remove the muffin tin from the oven and place it on a heat-resistant surface. Leave the muffins undisturbed for approximately 4 minutes so the egg structure firms slightly. Run a thin spatula or table knife around the perimeter of each muffin if required, then lift them from the tin.

Ready when: Each muffin releases intact, remains upright and has a tender but fully set centre.
Corrective actions
  • If: A muffin adheres strongly to the tin.
    Do: Loosen the full perimeter before lifting; do not pull forcefully from one side because the muffin may tear.
Step 9

Toast the seeded wholemeal bread

Toast
Heat source: ToasterSetting: Medium browning settingTime: 2–4 min

Place the approximately 90 g seeded wholemeal bread in the toaster. Select a medium browning setting and toast until the surfaces are crisp and golden-brown while the centres retain slight tenderness.

Ready when: The toast is evenly browned, crisp at the surface and shows no blackened or burnt areas.
Corrective actions
  • If: The toast darkens faster than expected.
    Do: Cancel the toaster cycle immediately and reduce the browning setting for any remaining slices.
  • If: The toast remains pale and soft after one cycle.
    Do: Return it to the toaster for a short additional cycle while monitoring closely.
Step 10

Check seasoning and divide into servings

Final seasoning and portioning
Time: 1–2 min

Taste a small piece from one representative muffin. If the seasoning is already defined and savoury, do not add the reserved salt. If noticeably under-seasoned, distribute up to the reserved 0.125 tsp fine salt very lightly across all 6 muffins. Divide the muffins into 2 portions of 3 muffins each and divide the toasted bread equally between the 2 servings.

Ready when: Each serving contains 3 egg muffins and approximately 45 g seeded wholemeal toast, with balanced seasoning and approximately equal total portion size.
Corrective actions
  • If: The muffins are adequately seasoned before the reserved salt is used.
    Do: Do not add any reserved salt.
Serving

Serve 3 warm egg muffins and approximately 45 g crisp seeded wholemeal toast per person. Keep the toast alongside rather than underneath the muffins so steam from the eggs does not soften it.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 3 days

Freezer: 1 months

Cool leftover egg muffins promptly, transfer to a sealed container and refrigerate at 5°C or below for up to 3 days. Freeze fully cooled muffins in a sealed freezer-safe container for up to 1 month. Store bread separately and toast fresh whenever practical. Reheat egg muffins only once and ensure they are steaming hot through the centre before eating.

Reheating

  • Air fryer: reheat refrigerated egg muffins at 160°C for approximately 5-7 minutes, until steaming hot through the centre without excessive surface browning.
  • Oven: reheat refrigerated egg muffins at 170°C for approximately 8-12 minutes, until steaming hot throughout.
  • From frozen: thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating using the air fryer or oven method.
  • Toast bread separately in the toaster immediately before serving.
