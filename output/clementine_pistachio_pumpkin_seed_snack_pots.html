Clementine, Pistachio & Pumpkin Seed Snack Pots

Portable morning snack pots combining fresh clementine segments with lightly toasted pistachios and pumpkin seeds, finished with a restrained maple-cinnamon glaze and a small amount of salt for contrast.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Wash the 2 whole clementines under cold running water and dry them thoroughly. Peel each clementine completely. Separate the fruit into individual segments. Remove loose white pith and discard it. Inspect the segments and remove any seeds encountered without excessively breaking the fruit.

Ready when: Two peeled clementines are separated into clean, intact segments with loose pith and seeds removed.

Prep 2

Inspect 32 g shelled pistachios and remove any shell fragments. Transfer to a chopping board and use the chef's knife to cut the nuts into irregular pieces approximately 4-6 mm across. Do not reduce them to crumbs.

Ready when: The pistachios are evenly coarse-chopped, with visible pieces large enough to retain crunch.

Prep 3

Weigh 20 g pumpkin seeds. Measure 1 tsp maple syrup, 0.25 tsp ground cinnamon and 0.0625 tsp fine salt into separate measured quantities so they can be added rapidly after toasting.

Ready when: All seed and seasoning components are accurately measured and immediately available.

Method

Step 1

Dry-toast the pistachios and pumpkin seeds

Toasted nut and seed topping
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: Low-mediumTime: 3–5 min

Place the dry frying pan over a low-to-medium gas flame. Add the prepared 32 g pistachios and 20 g pumpkin seeds in a single layer. Stir or shake the pan every 10-15 seconds so all surfaces heat evenly. Continue until the pumpkin seeds begin to produce occasional light popping sounds, the pistachios become slightly deeper green-gold and the mixture develops a distinctly toasted nut aroma.

Ready when: The nuts and seeds smell clearly toasted and aromatic, the pumpkin seeds are lightly golden in places, and no component shows blackened edges or produces smoke.
Corrective actions
  • If: The nuts or seeds darken rapidly or begin producing smoke.
    Do: Immediately remove the pan from the heat and transfer the contents to the mixing bowl. Discard any visibly blackened pieces.
  • If: Little aroma or colour develops after approximately 5 minutes.
    Do: Increase the gas flame slightly and continue in 20-second intervals while stirring continuously until the stated endpoint is achieved.
Step 2

Divide the clementine segments

Fruit preparation
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the prepared clementine segments equally between the 2 clean snack pots. Arrange the segments loosely rather than compressing them so the fruit retains its shape and released juice is minimised.

Ready when: Each snack pot contains approximately one clementine's worth of intact segments with no excessive pooling of juice.
Corrective actions
  • If: A clementine has released substantial juice during peeling.
    Do: Drain the loose juice before packing so the nut and seed topping is not unnecessarily softened during storage.
Step 3

Apply the maple-cinnamon seasoning

Toasted nut and seed topping
Time: 0.5–1 min

Immediately transfer the hot toasted pistachios and pumpkin seeds to the mixing bowl. Add 1 tsp maple syrup, 0.25 tsp ground cinnamon and 0.0625 tsp fine salt. Stir continuously for approximately 30-45 seconds until the maple syrup forms a very thin, even coating over the hot nuts and seeds and no concentrated wet patches remain.

Ready when: The nut-and-seed mixture is evenly lightly glazed, fragrant with cinnamon and remains predominantly loose rather than forming a sticky mass.
Corrective actions
  • If: The maple syrup collects in one part of the bowl or forms sticky clumps.
    Do: Continue stirring and separating the pieces with the spoon until the coating is redistributed while the mixture is still warm.
  • If: The mixture appears excessively wet after thorough stirring.
    Do: Spread it in a thin layer across the bowl and allow it to cool uncovered; do not add additional nuts or seeds because this would alter the specified quantities.
Step 4

Cool the toasted mixture completely

Cooling
Time: 6–8 min

Leave the seasoned pistachio and pumpkin seed mixture uncovered in the mixing bowl at room temperature. Stir once after approximately 3 minutes to break apart any pieces beginning to adhere. Do not seal or add the mixture to the fruit while it remains warm.

Ready when: The mixture has returned to room temperature, feels dry rather than tacky on the surface and the pieces separate readily when stirred.
Corrective actions
  • If: The mixture remains warm or tacky after 8 minutes.
    Do: Spread it more thinly in the bowl and continue cooling in 2-minute intervals until the stated endpoint is reached.
Step 5

Assemble the portable snack pots

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Place the bowl containing the cooled nut-and-seed mixture on the kitchen scales and determine its total finished mass. Divide the mixture equally between the 2 snack pots, placing approximately half over each portion of clementine. Fit the lids securely. Keep the pots upright during transport.

Ready when: Both pots contain equal portions of clementine and toasted nut-and-seed topping, the topping is cool before sealing, and the lids are fully secured for transport.
Corrective actions
  • If: One pot contains visibly more nut-and-seed mixture than the other.
    Do: Use the kitchen scales to transfer small amounts between pots until the topping is divided approximately equally.
  • If: The clementines have released visible juice during assembly.
    Do: Keep the toasted topping concentrated on the upper surface rather than mixing it through the fruit, limiting prolonged contact with the liquid.
Serving

Serve one chilled snack pot per person. For maximum textural contrast, stir only immediately before eating so the juicy clementine segments and crisp toasted pistachio-pumpkin seed mixture remain distinct for as long as possible.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 1 days

Refrigerate the assembled pots at 5°C or below and consume within 24 hours. The snack is suitable for transport to work or on a car journey; keep chilled in an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack if it will be outside refrigeration for an extended period. For maximum crunch, the toasted pistachio and pumpkin seed mixture may instead be stored separately in a sealed dry container and added immediately before eating.
