Title
Miso-Ginger Tofu, Broccoli & Brown Basmati Stir-Fry with Sesame and Edamame
Description
A high-protein dinner of crisp-edged tofu, broccoli and edamame tossed through a glossy miso-ginger sauce and served with nutty brown basmati rice. High wok heat develops browning before the sauce is added, while rice vinegar, ginger and toasted sesame keep the finished dish savoury, aromatic and balanced rather than heavy.

Storage

Refrigerator Days
2

Storage Instructions
Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate in sealed containers at 5°C or below for up to 2 days. For freezing, cool completely and freeze individual portions for up to 1 month; broccoli will soften somewhat after thawing. Reheat only once and ensure the tofu, rice, vegetables and sauce are steaming hot throughout before serving.

Reheating Methods

Hob: place one refrigerated serving in a covered frying pan or saucepan with 15-30 ml water. Heat over medium-low heat, stirring regularly, until steaming hot throughout and the sauce has returned to a glossy consistency.
Air fryer plus hob: reheat tofu and vegetables in the air fryer at 170°C until thoroughly hot while reheating the rice separately on the hob with 15-30 ml water. This method better restores some tofu surface texture.
From frozen: thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating using one of the stated methods.

Restaurant Quality Features

Fresh ginger is used generously so its aromatic heat remains identifiable after cooking.
Rice vinegar provides sufficient acidity to balance the fermented miso and sesame richness.
A restrained amount of honey rounds the sauce without turning the dish noticeably sweet.
Toasted sesame oil is incorporated in the sauce rather than used as the primary frying oil, preserving its aroma and avoiding unnecessary high-heat degradation.
Sesame seeds are toasted separately and added partly through the dish and partly as a finishing garnish for sustained nutty aroma and textural contrast.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete dinner serving including brown basmati rice, tofu, edamame, broccoli, cornflour, oils, sauce and sesame garnish.
The approximate 39 g protein per serving comes predominantly from tofu and edamame, with additional protein from broccoli, rice, miso and sesame.
The recipe is designed around the approximately 600 kcal dinner target; exact energy will vary with the nutritional composition of the selected tofu and supermarket condiments.
Brown basmati rice, broccoli and edamame provide substantial fibre and improve satiety relative to a refined-rice stir-fry.
Added cooking fat is deliberately restrained while sufficient oil is retained for tofu browning and characteristic sesame flavour.
Miso and soy sauce make sodium higher than in an unseasoned meal, so reduced-salt soy sauce is used and no additional salt is required.

Critical Success Factors

Dry the tofu thoroughly before applying cornflour; excess surface water will produce a pasty coating and inhibit browning.
Do not add the miso-ginger sauce until the tofu, broccoli and edamame have already reached their respective browning and cooking endpoints.
Ensure any water released from frozen edamame has evaporated before adding the sauce, otherwise the glaze will be diluted.
Stir the sauce immediately before pouring because cornflour settles rapidly.
Once the sauce is added, toss continuously and remove the wok from heat as soon as the sauce forms a glossy coating; prolonged cooking can scorch miso and soften the broccoli.
Toast sesame seeds separately under controlled low heat because they move quickly from aromatic to burnt.
Do not proceed to final plating until the brown basmati rice is fully tender and free from standing water.
