Kiwi, Greek Yoghurt & Hemp Seed Pots

Chilled evening snack pots combining thick Greek yoghurt with fresh lime and vanilla, ripe kiwi and lightly toasted hemp seeds. The kiwi provides bright acidity and juicy texture while the toasted hemp seeds add nutty aroma and gentle crunch.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Wash and dry the 2 whole kiwi fruit. Trim a thin slice from both ends of each fruit. Remove and discard the skin, then cut the edible flesh into pieces approximately 10 mm across. Transfer the prepared fruit to the small bowl.

Ready when: Approximately 150 g kiwi flesh is peeled and cut into evenly sized pieces with no skin remaining.

Prep 2

Wash and dry the whole lime. Finely grate approximately 1 g of the green outer zest, stopping before significant white pith is removed. Cut the lime and squeeze 10 ml juice, removing any seeds.

Ready when: Approximately 1 g lime zest and 10 ml seed-free lime juice are prepared.

Prep 3

Weigh 260 g Greek yoghurt, 20 g shelled hemp seeds and 10 g runny honey. Measure 0.5 tsp vanilla extract and 0.03125 tsp fine salt.

Ready when: All remaining ingredients are accurately measured and ready for immediate use.

Method

Step 1

Lightly toast the hemp seeds

Hemp seed topping
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: LowTime: 2–4 min

Place the dry frying pan over low gas heat. Add the 20 g shelled hemp seeds and spread them into an even layer. Stir or shake the pan continuously so the seeds heat evenly and do not remain stationary against the pan.

Ready when: The hemp seeds smell distinctly nutty, show slight golden colouring and remain loose, with no smoke or dark-brown scorched patches.
Corrective actions
  • If: The hemp seeds darken rapidly or produce smoke.
    Do: Remove the pan from the heat immediately and transfer the seeds to a cool plate or bowl.
  • If: No toasted aroma develops after 4 minutes.
    Do: Increase the flame slightly and continue stirring continuously in 20-second intervals until the stated endpoint is reached.
Step 2

Season the kiwi

Kiwi layer
Time: 2–5 min

Add approximately half of the prepared lime zest and 5 ml of the lime juice to the prepared kiwi. Fold gently 2-3 times to distribute the citrus without crushing the fruit. Leave the fruit undisturbed while the yoghurt base is prepared.

Ready when: The kiwi pieces remain structurally intact and appear lightly glossy with lime juice, with only a small amount of free fruit liquid in the bowl.
Corrective actions
  • If: The kiwi begins breaking down substantially during mixing.
    Do: Stop mixing immediately and leave the fruit undisturbed until assembly.
Step 3

Cool the toasted hemp seeds

Hemp seed topping
Time: 4–6 min

Remove the frying pan from direct heat and spread the toasted hemp seeds into a thin layer. Leave uncovered so heat and any residual steam can escape.

Ready when: The hemp seeds are at room temperature, dry and loose before they contact the cold yoghurt.
Corrective actions
  • If: The seeds remain warm after 6 minutes.
    Do: Transfer them to a cool plate and spread into a thinner layer until fully cooled.
Step 4

Prepare the lime-vanilla Greek yoghurt

Yoghurt base
Time: 1–2 min

Place 260 g Greek yoghurt in the mixing bowl. Add 10 g honey, the remaining approximately half of the lime zest, the remaining 5 ml lime juice, 0.5 tsp vanilla extract and 0.03125 tsp fine salt. Stir until completely homogeneous, scraping the base and sides of the bowl.

Ready when: The yoghurt is smooth, glossy and uniformly flavoured, with no concentrated honey streaks or unmixed zest.
Corrective actions
  • If: The yoghurt appears unusually stiff.
    Do: Continue stirring vigorously for approximately 20 seconds before making any adjustment; do not add unlisted liquid because the kiwi will supply additional moisture.
Step 5

Layer the evening snack pots

Assembly
Time: 3–5 min

Divide approximately half of the lime-vanilla yoghurt equally between the 2 snack pots. Divide the prepared kiwi, including any small quantity of released juice, equally over the yoghurt. Add the remaining yoghurt equally on top. Finish each pot with half of the completely cooled toasted hemp seeds. Fit the lids securely.

Ready when: Both pots contain approximately equal quantities of yoghurt, kiwi and hemp seeds, with visible fruit layers and dry toasted seeds on the surface.
Corrective actions
  • If: One pot is visibly heavier than the other.
    Do: Use the kitchen scales to transfer small amounts between pots until both filled weights are approximately equal.
  • If: The toasted hemp seeds are still warm.
    Do: Do not seal the pots. Remove or hold the seeds separately until they have cooled completely.
Step 6

Chill before serving

Chilling
Heat source: RefrigeratorTemperature: 5°CTime: 10 min minimum

Transfer the sealed pots to a refrigerator operating at 5°C or below and chill for at least 10 minutes before serving.

Ready when: The pots are fully chilled, the yoghurt remains thick and the kiwi retains identifiable pieces rather than breaking down into the dairy.
Corrective actions
  • If: A noticeable layer of kiwi juice accumulates during chilling.
    Do: Fold the juice gently into the immediately adjacent yoghurt immediately before eating rather than discarding it.
Serving

Serve one chilled pot per person. Eat through the layers rather than completely mixing at the outset so each spoonful can retain contrast between thick lime-vanilla yoghurt, juicy kiwi and nutty toasted hemp seeds.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 1 days

Store the covered pots continuously at 5°C or below and consume within 24 hours for best texture. Kiwi contains enzymes that can gradually alter dairy texture during prolonged storage, so these pots are best assembled on the day they are eaten. For maximum hemp-seed crunch, the cooled toasted seeds may be kept separately in a small dry container and scattered over immediately before serving.
