Miso-Sesame Tofu & Edamame Soba Noodle Salad with Broccoli and Carrot

Recipe info

A portable Japanese-inspired soba salad with crisp-edged sesame tofu, edamame, tender-crisp broccoli and raw carrot, coated in a punchy miso, ginger, soy and rice-vinegar dressing.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Drain the tofu and pat it thoroughly dry. Cut it into approximately 20 mm cubes. In the large mixing bowl, gently toss the tofu with 1 tsp reduced-salt soy sauce and 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil, then sprinkle over 2 tsp cornflour and toss until each piece has a thin, dry-looking coating.

Ready when: The tofu cubes are evenly coated with no visible wet pools of soy sauce and no large clumps of cornflour.

Prep 2

Cut the broccoli into small florets approximately 25-30 mm across. Peel any tough outer skin from the tender stem and slice the usable stem thinly. Trim the carrot, then cut it into fine matchsticks approximately 3 mm thick and 40-50 mm long.

Ready when: The broccoli pieces are small enough to cook rapidly and evenly, and the carrot is cut into thin raw matchsticks.

Prep 3

Finely grate 2 tsp ginger and the peeled garlic clove. Keep them ready with 1 tbsp white miso, the remaining 1 tsp reduced-salt soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, the remaining 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tsp runny honey and 1 tbsp hot water.

Ready when: All dressing ingredients are measured and ready to combine.

Method

Step 1

Air-fry the tofu

Tofu
Heat source: air_fryerSetting: 200°CTemperature: 200°CTime: 11–14 min

Preheat the air fryer to 200°C if your model benefits from preheating. Arrange the coated tofu in a single layer with space for air circulation. Air-fry at 200°C for approximately 11-14 minutes, shaking or turning once after about 7 minutes.

Ready when: The tofu is piping hot, dry on the surface and golden at several edges, with a lightly crisp exterior and tender centre.
Corrective actions
  • If: The tofu is browning very rapidly before the surface has crisped.
    Do: Reduce the air-fryer temperature to 190°C for the remaining cooking time.
  • If: The tofu is still pale and soft after 14 minutes.
    Do: Continue air-frying for 2-3 minutes, checking every minute, until distinct golden edges develop.
Step 2

Boil the water

Noodles and vegetables
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: highTime: 2–3 min

While the tofu cooks, boil enough water in the kettle to generously cover the soba noodles in the saucepan. Pour the boiling water into the saucepan and bring it back to a steady boil over high heat.

Ready when: The saucepan contains vigorously boiling water ready for the noodles.
Step 3

Cook the soba, edamame and broccoli

Noodles and vegetables
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: medium-highTime: 4–6 min

Add the soba noodles to the boiling water and cook according to the lower end of the package timing, normally about 4-5 minutes. Add the frozen edamame approximately 3 minutes before the noodles are expected to be ready, then add the broccoli approximately 2 minutes before the end. Stir gently after each addition so the noodles do not stick together.

Ready when: The soba is tender but retains a slight bite, the edamame is hot throughout, and the broccoli is bright green and tender-crisp rather than soft.
Corrective actions
  • If: The noodles reach the correct texture before the broccoli is tender-crisp.
    Do: Drain the noodles and edamame with tongs or a sieve insert if practical, then allow the broccoli another 30-60 seconds before draining.
  • If: The broccoli begins losing its bright green colour.
    Do: Drain immediately; it is approaching overcooked.
Step 4

Rinse and drain thoroughly

Noodles and vegetables
Time: 2–3 min

Drain the noodles, edamame and broccoli immediately into the colander. Rinse thoroughly under cold running water while gently separating the noodles with clean fingers or tongs until the mixture is cool. Shake the colander firmly and leave it to drain for approximately 1 minute.

Ready when: The noodles and vegetables are cool and well drained, with no free water collecting beneath the colander.
Corrective actions
  • If: The noodles still feel slippery with loose surface starch.
    Do: Rinse for another 20-30 seconds while gently separating the strands.
  • If: Water continues dripping heavily after rinsing.
    Do: Shake the colander again and allow another minute of draining before dressing the salad.
Step 5

Whisk the miso-sesame dressing

Dressing
Time: 1–2 min

Once the tofu has been transferred to the air fryer, use the same mixing bowl to combine 1 tbsp white miso, 1 tsp reduced-salt soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tsp runny honey, the grated ginger, grated garlic and 1 tbsp hot water. Whisk or stir vigorously until completely smooth.

Ready when: The dressing is smooth, glossy and pourable, with a balanced salty, savoury, acidic and lightly sweet flavour.
Corrective actions
  • If: The dressing remains too thick to coat noodles evenly.
    Do: Add 1 tsp hot water and mix again; repeat once only if necessary.
  • If: The dressing tastes excessively sharp.
    Do: Add up to an additional 1/2 tsp runny honey.
Step 6

Dress the noodle salad

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Add the well-drained soba, edamame and broccoli to the dressing bowl with the raw carrot matchsticks. Add 1 tsp of the sesame seeds and toss thoroughly with tongs until the noodles and vegetables are evenly coated and no concentrated patches of miso dressing remain.

Ready when: The salad is evenly dressed but not wet, the noodle strands remain separate, and the carrot retains a crisp raw texture.
Corrective actions
  • If: A watery pool forms at the bottom of the bowl.
    Do: Transfer the salad briefly back to the colander, drain the excess liquid, then return it to the bowl.
  • If: The noodles seem dry despite being evenly coated.
    Do: Add 1 tsp water and toss again rather than adding more oil.
Step 7

Finish with the crisp tofu

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Allow the cooked tofu to stand for approximately 2 minutes so excess steam can escape. If serving immediately, fold the tofu gently through the salad and scatter over the remaining 1 tsp sesame seeds. If packing for later, keep the tofu in a separate dry compartment or container and add it immediately before eating.

Ready when: The salad has distinct tender noodles, crisp carrot, tender-crisp broccoli and golden tofu, with the tofu remaining as dry and crisp as practical.
Corrective actions
  • If: The tofu is visibly steaming when packing for later.
    Do: Leave it uncovered for another 2-3 minutes before sealing so condensation does not soften the coating.
Serving

Divide the dressed soba, edamame, broccoli and carrot evenly between two bowls or lunch containers. Add half of the crisp tofu to each serving and finish with the remaining sesame seeds. For packed lunches, transport chilled and keep the tofu separate until eating for the best texture.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Cool all cooked components promptly before sealing. Refrigerate the dressed noodle salad for up to 2 days. For best texture, refrigerate the air-fried tofu in a separate sealed container and combine immediately before eating. For transport to work or on a car journey, carry the salad chilled in an insulated bag with an ice pack and refrigerate on arrival where possible.
