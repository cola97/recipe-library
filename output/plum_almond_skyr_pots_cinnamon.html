Plum & Almond Skyr Pots with Cinnamon

Portable morning snack pots of thick plain skyr layered with lightly macerated fresh plum and finished with cinnamon-toasted almonds. Lemon and a restrained amount of honey sharpen the fruit while the toasted nuts provide crisp contrast against the cool dairy.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Wash the 2 whole plums under cold running water and dry thoroughly. Cut each plum in half along its natural seam, remove and discard the stone, then cut the usable flesh into approximately 10 mm pieces.

Ready when: Approximately 150 g plum flesh is clean, stone-free and cut into evenly sized pieces.

Prep 2

Inspect 24 g almonds and remove any shell fragments. Transfer to the chopping board and roughly chop into pieces approximately 4-6 mm across, avoiding excessive fine crumbs.

Ready when: The almonds are evenly coarse-chopped and retain pieces large enough to provide crunch.

Prep 3

Wash and dry the lemon. Finely grate approximately 1 g of yellow outer zest without removing significant white pith. Cut the lemon and squeeze 10 ml juice, removing all seeds.

Ready when: Approximately 1 g lemon zest and 10 ml seed-free lemon juice are prepared.

Prep 4

Weigh 240 g plain skyr and 10 g honey. Measure 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon, allocating 0.25 tsp to the plum and 0.25 tsp to the almonds. Measure 0.5 tsp vanilla extract and 0.03125 tsp fine salt.

Ready when: All remaining ingredients are accurately measured and allocated to their intended components.

Method

Step 1

Macerate the plum

Plum layer
Time: 5–8 min

Place the prepared plum pieces in the small bowl. Add 10 g honey, the measured 10 ml lemon juice, approximately 1 g lemon zest and 0.25 tsp ground cinnamon. Fold gently until all fruit surfaces are lightly coated. Leave uncovered while the remaining components are prepared.

Ready when: The plum pieces remain structurally intact but have released a small amount of glossy, lightly syrupy juice in the base of the bowl.
Corrective actions
  • If: The plum pieces begin collapsing substantially during mixing.
    Do: Stop stirring and allow the mixture to rest undisturbed so the remaining fruit retains its shape.
  • If: No juice is released after 8 minutes.
    Do: Press approximately one-quarter of the plum pieces once against the side of the bowl with the back of a spoon, then fold them back through the mixture.
Step 2

Toast the almonds

Almond topping
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: Low-mediumTime: 3–5 min

Place the dry frying pan over low-to-medium gas heat. Add the prepared 24 g chopped almonds in a single layer. Stir or shake the pan every 10-15 seconds to expose all pieces evenly to the hot surface.

Ready when: The almonds smell distinctly nutty and show light golden-brown colour on multiple cut surfaces, with no smoke or blackened edges.
Corrective actions
  • If: The almonds darken rapidly or begin producing smoke.
    Do: Remove the pan from the heat immediately and transfer the almonds to a cool plate or bowl.
  • If: Little aroma or colour develops after 5 minutes.
    Do: Increase the flame slightly and continue stirring continuously in 20-second intervals until the stated endpoint is reached.
Step 3

Season and cool the almonds

Almond topping
Time: 4–6 min

Remove the frying pan from direct heat. Immediately add the remaining 0.25 tsp cinnamon and 0.03125 tsp fine salt to the hot almonds. Toss continuously for approximately 20-30 seconds until the seasoning is evenly distributed, then spread the almonds into a single layer in the pan and leave uncovered to cool.

Ready when: The almonds are evenly seasoned, no longer hot and remain dry and loose rather than damp or clumped.
Corrective actions
  • If: Cinnamon accumulates in concentrated patches.
    Do: Continue tossing while the almonds are still warm until the seasoning is distributed evenly.
  • If: The almonds remain warm after 6 minutes.
    Do: Transfer them to a cool plate and spread into a thinner layer until they reach room temperature.
Step 4

Prepare the vanilla skyr

Skyr base
Time: 1–2 min

Place 240 g plain skyr in the mixing bowl. Add 0.5 tsp vanilla extract and stir until completely homogeneous, scraping the sides and base of the bowl to eliminate unmixed streaks.

Ready when: The skyr is smooth and uniformly scented with vanilla, with no visible unmixed areas.
Corrective actions
  • If: The skyr appears unusually stiff.
    Do: Continue stirring vigorously for approximately 20 seconds; do not add unlisted liquid because the plum juices will loosen the finished pot.
Step 5

Layer the snack pots

Assembly
Time: 3–5 min

Divide approximately half of the vanilla skyr equally between the 2 snack pots. Divide the macerated plum mixture, including all released juices, equally over the skyr. Add the remaining skyr equally on top. Finish each pot with half of the fully cooled cinnamon-toasted almonds. Fit the lids securely.

Ready when: Both pots contain approximately equal quantities of skyr, plum and almonds, and the almond topping is fully cool before the containers are sealed.
Corrective actions
  • If: One pot is visibly heavier than the other.
    Do: Use the kitchen scales to transfer small amounts between containers until both portions are approximately equal.
  • If: The toasted almonds are still warm.
    Do: Do not seal the pots; remove the almonds and allow them to cool completely before final assembly.
Step 6

Chill the finished pots

Chilling and transport
Heat source: RefrigeratorTemperature: 5°CTime: 10 min minimum

Transfer the sealed snack pots immediately to a refrigerator operating at 5°C or below. Chill until required for transport or consumption.

Ready when: The pots are fully chilled, the skyr remains thick and the fruit juices have formed a light plum ripple without excessive free liquid.
Corrective actions
  • If: Substantial fruit liquid accumulates after storage.
    Do: Stir the plum layer gently into the adjacent skyr immediately before eating rather than discarding the liquid.
Serving

Serve one chilled pot per person. Eat through the layers rather than fully mixing at the outset so each spoonful can retain contrast between tangy skyr, juicy cinnamon-plum and crisp toasted almonds.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Store the assembled pots at 5°C or below and consume within 2 days. For maximum almond crunch, the cooled toasted almonds may instead be carried in a separate small dry container and added immediately before eating. For work or car transport, use an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack whenever continuous refrigeration is unavailable.
