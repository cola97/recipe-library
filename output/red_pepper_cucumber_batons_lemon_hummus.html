Red Pepper & Cucumber Batons with Lemon Hummus

Recipe info

A crisp, portable snack of sweet red pepper and cool cucumber batons with creamy hummus sharpened with fresh lemon juice, packed separately to preserve texture.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Halve the washed red pepper, remove the stem, seeds and white membranes, then cut the flesh into batons approximately 10 mm wide and 60-70 mm long.

Ready when: The pepper is cut into firm, similarly sized batons that are easy to dip.

Prep 2

Trim both ends from the washed cucumber, then cut it into batons approximately 10 mm thick and 60-70 mm long, retaining the edible skin.

Ready when: The cucumber is cut into firm, evenly sized batons with minimal exposed watery surface.

Prep 3

Measure 130 g hummus, 1 tbsp lemon juice and 1/8 tsp ground black pepper.

Ready when: The hummus seasoning ingredients are accurately measured and ready to combine.

Method

Step 1

Make the lemon hummus

Hummus
Time: 0.5–1 min

Combine the 130 g hummus with 1 tbsp lemon juice and 1/8 tsp ground black pepper. Stir thoroughly for approximately 30 seconds until smooth and evenly seasoned.

Ready when: The hummus tastes distinctly brighter and more lemony while remaining thick enough to cling to a vegetable baton.
Corrective actions
  • If: The hummus becomes looser than ideal for dipping.
    Do: Refrigerate it until required rather than adding more liquid.
Step 2

Portion the lemon hummus

Packing
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the lemon hummus evenly between 2 small sealed sauce pots, giving approximately 65 g per serving.

Ready when: Each snack contains an equal, securely sealed portion of lemon hummus.
Step 3

Pack the vegetable batons

Packing
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the red pepper and cucumber batons evenly between the two snack containers. Keep the vegetable compartment separate from the sealed hummus pot so the vegetables remain crisp and the hummus cannot coat them during transport.

Ready when: Each snack contains an even mixture of crisp red pepper and cucumber batons with a separate portion of lemon hummus.
Corrective actions
  • If: The cucumber surfaces are visibly wet after cutting.
    Do: Pat the batons dry before packing so excess moisture does not collect in the container.
Serving

At the time of eating, dip the red pepper and cucumber batons into the lemon hummus. Keep the vegetables separate until each bite is assembled so they remain crisp and fresh.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 1 days

Refrigerate the lemon hummus and prepared vegetables promptly and use within 24 hours for the best texture. Keep the hummus sealed separately from the vegetable batons. For transport to work or on a car journey, carry the snack in an insulated bag with an ice pack and keep chilled until eating.
