Cannellini Bean, Cottage Cheese & Roasted Courgette Wholewheat Pasta Salad with Basil and Lemon

Recipe info

A portable high-protein wholewheat pasta salad with caramelised roasted courgette, creamy cottage cheese and cannellini beans, brightened with lemon, basil and Dijon mustard.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Trim the ends from the washed courgettes, then cut the courgettes into half-moons approximately 10 mm thick.

Ready when: The courgette pieces are evenly sized so they roast at a similar rate.

Prep 2

Drain the cannellini beans, rinse them thoroughly under cold running water and leave them in the colander until no free water remains.

Ready when: The beans are clean-tasting and well drained rather than wet.

Prep 3

Finely grate the zest from approximately half of the lemon, then halve the lemon and squeeze the juice. Peel and finely grate the garlic clove. Tear or roughly chop the basil leaves immediately before assembly.

Ready when: The lemon zest, juice, garlic and basil are ready without bruising the basil excessively.

Method

Step 1

Roast the courgettes

Courgette
Heat source: air_fryerSetting: 200°CTemperature: 200°CTime: 12–15 min

Toss the courgette half-moons with 1 1/2 tsp olive oil, 1/4 tsp fine salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Arrange them in the air-fryer basket with enough space for air circulation. Air-fry at 200°C for approximately 12-15 minutes, shaking or turning once after approximately 7 minutes.

Ready when: The courgettes are tender through the centre with distinct golden-brown edges and no pool of released water.
Corrective actions
  • If: The courgettes are browning rapidly while remaining firm inside.
    Do: Reduce the air-fryer temperature to 185°C and continue until tender.
  • If: The courgettes remain pale and watery after 15 minutes.
    Do: Continue air-frying for 2-3 minutes, checking every minute, until the moisture has evaporated and browned edges develop.
Step 2

Cook the wholewheat pasta

Pasta
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: medium-highTime: 8–10 min

While the courgettes roast, boil sufficient water in the kettle and pour it into the saucepan. Return it to a vigorous boil, add the 90 g wholewheat pasta and cook according to the lower end of the package timing, normally approximately 8-10 minutes.

Ready when: The pasta is tender throughout but retains a slight bite and has not become soft or swollen.
Corrective actions
  • If: The pasta reaches the correct texture before the stated package time.
    Do: Drain it immediately rather than continuing to cook by the clock.
Step 3

Cool and drain the pasta

Pasta
Time: 2–3 min

Drain the pasta immediately into the colander and rinse briefly under cold running water while tossing gently until the pasta is cool enough for a chilled salad. Shake the colander thoroughly and leave the pasta to drain for approximately 1 minute.

Ready when: The pasta is cool, separate and well drained, with no visible water collecting beneath the colander.
Corrective actions
  • If: The pasta remains visibly wet after rinsing.
    Do: Shake the colander again and allow another minute of draining before adding it to the dressing.
Step 4

Make the basil-lemon dressing

Dressing
Time: 1–2 min

In the mixing bowl, combine 2 tbsp lemon juice, the prepared lemon zest, 2 tsp Dijon mustard, the remaining 1/2 tsp olive oil, the grated garlic, the remaining 1/8 tsp fine salt and the remaining 1/4 tsp black pepper. Stir vigorously until evenly combined.

Ready when: The dressing is sharp, savoury and lightly emulsified, with no concentrated mustard or garlic pockets.
Corrective actions
  • If: The dressing tastes excessively sharp.
    Do: Add 1 tsp cold water and mix again before making any further adjustment.
Step 5

Dress the pasta and beans

Assembly
Time: 1–2 min

Add the well-drained pasta and cannellini beans to the dressing. Add approximately half of the basil and fold thoroughly until the pasta and beans are evenly coated.

Ready when: The pasta and beans are evenly coated but there is no watery dressing pooling at the bottom of the bowl.
Corrective actions
  • If: Liquid collects at the bottom of the bowl.
    Do: Allow the mixture to stand for 1 minute and fold again; if excess liquid remains, drain it off before adding the cottage cheese.
Step 6

Add the roasted courgette and cottage cheese

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Allow the roasted courgette to cool for approximately 2 minutes so it is no longer steaming strongly. Add it to the dressed pasta with the 300 g cottage cheese and remaining basil. Fold gently until the cottage cheese is distributed through the salad while retaining visible curds and intact courgette pieces.

Ready when: The salad is creamy and evenly seasoned, with distinct beans, browned courgette, pasta and cottage-cheese curds rather than a uniform paste.
Corrective actions
  • If: The salad becomes loose or watery after the cottage cheese is added.
    Do: Leave it uncovered for 2-3 minutes before packing so excess surface moisture can dissipate.
Step 7

Pack and chill

Packing
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the salad evenly between the two lunch containers. Leave the lids slightly ajar only until any residual warmth has dissipated, then seal and refrigerate promptly.

Ready when: Each container holds an even portion of salad and is chilled promptly without trapping visible steam.
Serving

Serve chilled or cool. Stir lightly before eating to redistribute the lemon-basil dressing while keeping the roasted courgette pieces and cottage-cheese curds distinct.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Refrigerate promptly in sealed containers and use within 2 days. For transport to work or on a car journey, keep the salad in an insulated bag with an ice pack and refrigerate on arrival where possible. Keep chilled until shortly before eating.
