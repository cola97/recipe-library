Title
Smoked Paprika Roasted Chickpeas with Cucumber Sticks
Description
A portable afternoon snack of crisp roasted chickpeas coated with smoked paprika, cumin and lemon zest, served with cool cucumber sticks for fresh contrast. The chickpeas are dried thoroughly before roasting and packed separately from the cucumber to preserve crunch.

Mise En Place

Item 1
Prep Id
PREP_01

Ingredient Ids

chickpeas_canned
Instruction
Open the chickpea tin and pour the contents into the colander, discarding all canning liquid. Rinse thoroughly under cold running water while gently agitating the chickpeas. Drain for approximately 2 minutes, then spread on clean kitchen paper and roll or blot gently until the surfaces are as dry as practical. Remove any loose chickpea skins that detach during drying.
Endpoint
Approximately 220 g drained chickpeas are clean, externally dry and free from visible surface water.
Item 2
Prep Id
PREP_02

Ingredient Ids

cucumber
Instruction
Wash and dry the whole cucumber. Cut approximately half to provide about 220 g. Cut into sticks approximately 10 mm x 10 mm x 70 mm. If the centre is unusually soft and watery, trim away only the softest seed core. Pat the cut surfaces dry with clean kitchen paper.
Endpoint
Approximately 220 g cucumber is cut into crisp, dry sticks suitable for transport.
Item 3
Prep Id
PREP_03

Ingredient Ids

lemon
Instruction
Wash and dry the lemon. Finely grate approximately 1 g of the yellow outer zest, stopping before significant white pith is removed.
Endpoint
Approximately 1 g fine lemon zest is prepared.

Storage Instructions
For best texture, refrigerate the cucumber at 5°C or below and store the fully cooled roasted chickpeas in a separate sealed dry compartment. The complete snack is suitable for work or car transport; use an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack if the cucumber will be outside refrigeration for an extended period. Roasted chickpeas may soften slightly under refrigeration, so the crispest texture is obtained within 24 hours.

Restaurant Quality Features

Chickpeas are rinsed and dried thoroughly before roasting, allowing the surfaces to crisp rather than steam.
A measured amount of olive oil provides sufficient surface coating for browning and spice adhesion without unnecessarily increasing calories.
Smoked paprika and cumin provide layered warm spice character, while garlic granules contribute savoury depth without introducing moisture.
Lemon zest adds aromatic acidity without wetting the roasted chickpeas and compromising crispness.
Seasoning is applied while the chickpeas are hot so the spices adhere evenly to the residual oil.
The chickpeas are cooled uncovered before packing, preventing trapped steam from immediately softening the roasted exterior.
Cucumber is packed separately so its moisture does not compromise the chickpea texture.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete afternoon snack serving including roasted chickpeas, cucumber, olive oil and all seasonings.
The recipe is designed around the approximately 200 kcal afternoon-snack target.
The general 30 g protein requirement does not apply to afternoon snacks under the specified meal-plan rules; approximately 8 g protein per serving is therefore compliant.
Chickpeas provide plant protein, carbohydrate and substantial dietary fibre, while cucumber adds volume and hydration with minimal additional energy.
Added oil is deliberately limited while remaining sufficient to support roasting and spice adhesion.
Nutrition values are estimates and will vary with the exact drained weight and nutritional composition of the chickpeas used.
The recipe follows the supplied structured-recipe and afternoon-snack requirements.

Critical Success Factors

Dry the rinsed chickpeas extremely thoroughly before adding oil; residual water is the main cause of soft roasted chickpeas.
Do not add wet acidic ingredients such as lemon juice to the roasted chickpeas before storage; use zest for brightness without sacrificing crispness.
Judge completion by actual crispness of a briefly cooled chickpea rather than roasting time alone.
Apply dry spices immediately after roasting while the chickpeas remain hot and lightly oil-coated.
Cool chickpeas completely before sealing them in any container.
Keep cucumber physically separate from the chickpeas throughout storage and transport.
