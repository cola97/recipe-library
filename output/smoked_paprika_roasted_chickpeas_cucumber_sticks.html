Smoked Paprika Roasted Chickpeas with Cucumber Sticks

Recipe info

A portable afternoon snack of crisp roasted chickpeas coated with smoked paprika, cumin and lemon zest, served with cool cucumber sticks for fresh contrast. The chickpeas are dried thoroughly before roasting and packed separately from the cucumber to preserve crunch.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Open the chickpea tin and pour the contents into the colander, discarding all canning liquid. Rinse thoroughly under cold running water while gently agitating the chickpeas. Drain for approximately 2 minutes, then spread on clean kitchen paper and roll or blot gently until the surfaces are as dry as practical. Remove any loose chickpea skins that detach during drying.

Ready when: Approximately 220 g drained chickpeas are clean, externally dry and free from visible surface water.

Prep 2

Wash and dry the whole cucumber. Cut approximately half to provide about 220 g. Cut into sticks approximately 10 mm x 10 mm x 70 mm. If the centre is unusually soft and watery, trim away only the softest seed core. Pat the cut surfaces dry with clean kitchen paper.

Ready when: Approximately 220 g cucumber is cut into crisp, dry sticks suitable for transport.

Prep 3

Wash and dry the lemon. Finely grate approximately 1 g of the yellow outer zest, stopping before significant white pith is removed.

Ready when: Approximately 1 g fine lemon zest is prepared.

Prep 4

Measure 8 g olive oil, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 0.5 tsp ground cumin, 0.5 tsp garlic granules, 0.1875 tsp initial fine salt, 0.0625 tsp reserved fine salt and 0.125 tsp black pepper.

Ready when: All roasting and seasoning ingredients are accurately measured and ready for immediate use.

Method

Step 1

Preheat the air fryer

Air fryer preparation
Heat source: Air fryerSetting: 190°CTemperature: 190°CTime: 3–5 min

Preheat the air fryer to 190°C. Allow the appliance to complete its preheat cycle or operate empty at the set temperature for approximately 3 minutes before loading the chickpeas.

Ready when: The air fryer has reached 190°C and is ready to receive the chickpeas.
Corrective actions
  • If: The air fryer has not reached operating temperature after 5 minutes.
    Do: Confirm the temperature setting and allow an additional 2 minutes before proceeding.
Step 2

Coat the dried chickpeas

Chickpea preparation
Time: 1–2 min

Place the thoroughly dried 220 g chickpeas in the mixing bowl. Add 8 g olive oil and toss until every chickpea has a thin visible sheen and no oil remains pooled in the base of the bowl.

Ready when: The chickpeas are evenly and lightly coated with oil without visible pooling.
Corrective actions
  • If: Water becomes visible in the base of the bowl during tossing.
    Do: Transfer the chickpeas back to clean kitchen paper, dry thoroughly again, clean and dry the bowl, then resume coating.
Step 3

Roast the chickpeas until dry and crisp

Roasting
Heat source: Air fryerSetting: 190°CTemperature: 190°CTime: 14–20 min

Transfer the oiled chickpeas to the preheated air-fryer basket and spread them into the most even layer practical. Roast at 190°C. Shake the basket thoroughly after approximately 6 minutes and again every 4-5 minutes thereafter so multiple surfaces are exposed to hot circulating air.

Ready when: The chickpeas are dry on the exterior, lightly golden, audibly firm when shaken against the basket and crisp when one is cooled briefly and bitten, without burnt or bitter surfaces.
Corrective actions
  • If: The chickpeas brown rapidly while their centres remain soft.
    Do: Reduce the air-fryer temperature to 175°C and continue in 3-minute intervals, shaking between intervals.
  • If: The chickpeas remain soft after 20 minutes without excessive browning.
    Do: Continue roasting at 180°C in 3-minute intervals, checking one cooled chickpea after each interval.
  • If: Individual chickpeas become dark brown or black.
    Do: Remove the darkest chickpeas immediately and reduce the temperature before continuing.
Step 4

Apply the smoked paprika seasoning

Seasoning
Time: 0.5–1 min

Immediately transfer the hot roasted chickpeas to the clean mixing bowl. Add 1 tsp smoked paprika, 0.5 tsp ground cumin, 0.5 tsp garlic granules, approximately 1 g lemon zest, 0.1875 tsp fine salt and 0.125 tsp black pepper. Toss continuously for approximately 30-45 seconds so the residual oil and heat distribute the dry seasoning evenly.

Ready when: The chickpeas have an even red-orange seasoning coat with no concentrated spice patches or visible loose seasoning collected in one area.
Corrective actions
  • If: Seasoning accumulates in dry patches.
    Do: Continue tossing vigorously while the chickpeas are still hot until the coating becomes more uniform.
  • If: The paprika smells scorched or acrid.
    Do: Transfer the chickpeas immediately to a cool plate and stop further heating.
Step 5

Cool the roasted chickpeas completely

Cooling
Time: 8–12 min

Spread the seasoned chickpeas into a single layer across the bowl or a clean plate and leave uncovered at room temperature. Stir or shake once halfway through cooling so trapped steam can escape.

Ready when: The chickpeas are at room temperature, externally dry and remain crisp rather than steaming or tacky.
Corrective actions
  • If: The chickpeas remain warm or soften from retained steam.
    Do: Spread them into a thinner layer and continue cooling uncovered for an additional 5 minutes.
Step 6

Prepare and dry the cucumber sticks

Vegetable preparation
Time: 4–6 min

Complete the cucumber preparation specified in mise en place. Inspect the cut sticks for excessive surface moisture and blot thoroughly with clean kitchen paper before packing.

Ready when: The cucumber sticks are evenly sized, cold or room temperature, crisp and externally dry.
Corrective actions
  • If: Visible cucumber liquid collects after cutting.
    Do: Spread the sticks on clean kitchen paper for 2 minutes, then blot again before packing.
Step 7

Check the chickpea seasoning

Final seasoning
Time: 1–2 min

Taste one cooled chickpea. If the smoked paprika, cumin and salt are clearly defined, do not add the reserved salt. If noticeably under-seasoned, add up to the reserved 0.0625 tsp fine salt and toss thoroughly.

Ready when: The chickpeas taste smoky, savoury and well seasoned without excessive saltiness.
Corrective actions
  • If: The chickpeas are adequately seasoned before the reserved salt is used.
    Do: Do not add the reserved salt.
Step 8

Portion the portable snack

Packing
Time: 2–4 min

Divide the completely cooled roasted chickpeas equally between 2 dry snack compartments or small inner containers. Divide the cucumber sticks equally between separate compartments. Do not allow cucumber to contact the chickpeas during storage. Seal all containers securely.

Ready when: Each serving contains approximately half of the chickpeas and half of the cucumber, with the dry roasted chickpeas physically separated from the moist vegetables.
Corrective actions
  • If: One serving contains visibly more chickpeas than the other.
    Do: Use the kitchen scales to transfer small amounts until both chickpea portions are approximately equal.
  • If: Moisture is visible in the chickpea compartment before sealing.
    Do: Dry the compartment completely and confirm the chickpeas are fully cool before repacking.
Serving

Serve one complete snack portion per person. Eat the crisp smoked-paprika chickpeas alternately with the cold cucumber sticks rather than mixing them together, preserving the contrast between dry crunch and fresh juiciness.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

For best texture, refrigerate the cucumber at 5°C or below and store the fully cooled roasted chickpeas in a separate sealed dry compartment. The complete snack is suitable for work or car transport; use an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack if the cucumber will be outside refrigeration for an extended period. Roasted chickpeas may soften slightly under refrigeration, so the crispest texture is obtained within 24 hours.
