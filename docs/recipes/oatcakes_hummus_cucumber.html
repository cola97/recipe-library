Oatcakes with Hummus and Cucumber

Recipe info

A simple portable snack of crisp wholegrain oatcakes with creamy hummus and cool cucumber, packed separately so the oatcakes stay crunchy until eating.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Trim both ends from the washed cucumber. Cut the cucumber into batons approximately 10 mm thick and 60-70 mm long, retaining the edible skin.

Ready when: The cucumber is cut into firm, easy-to-dip batons of similar size.

Prep 2

Weigh 80 g hummus and measure 1/4 tsp smoked paprika.

Ready when: The hummus and paprika are ready to portion.

Method

Step 1

Season the hummus

Hummus
Time: 1 min

Stir 1/4 tsp smoked paprika through the 80 g hummus until the colour is even and no concentrated pockets of spice remain.

Ready when: The hummus is evenly seasoned and remains thick enough to hold on an oatcake or cucumber baton.
Step 2

Portion the hummus

Packing
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the seasoned hummus evenly between 2 small sealed sauce pots, giving approximately 40 g per serving.

Ready when: Each serving contains an equal portion of hummus in a securely sealed compartment.
Step 3

Pack the cucumber and oatcakes

Packing
Time: 2–3 min

Divide the cucumber batons evenly between the 2 snack containers. Add 2 oatcakes per serving in a completely dry compartment or separate dry bag, ensuring they do not contact the cucumber or hummus during storage.

Ready when: Each snack contains 2 dry oatcakes, an equal portion of cucumber batons and a separate sealed hummus portion.
Corrective actions
  • If: The container has no dry compartment for the oatcakes.
    Do: Pack the oatcakes in a separate small reusable container or sealed food bag.
Serving

At the time of eating, spread or dip the oatcakes into the smoked-paprika hummus and eat the cucumber batons alongside or dipped into the remaining hummus. Keep the oatcakes dry until each bite is assembled so they remain crisp.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 1 days

Refrigerate the hummus and cucumber promptly and use within 24 hours for the best cucumber texture. Keep the oatcakes sealed separately at room temperature so they remain crisp. For transport to work or on a car journey, carry the hummus and cucumber in an insulated bag with an ice pack and keep chilled until eating.
