Herb-Roasted Seitan with Crispy Potatoes, Carrots, Savoy Cabbage and Mushroom-Onion Gravy

Recipe info

A high-protein vegetarian roast dinner with steamed then herb-roasted seitan, crisp golden potatoes, caramelised carrots, tender Savoy cabbage and a deeply savoury mushroom-onion gravy.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Cut the potatoes into approximately 20 mm chunks, retaining clean edible skins. Trim the carrots and cut them into batons approximately 15 mm thick and 50 mm long. Remove the hard core from the Savoy cabbage, then slice the leaves into ribbons approximately 10 mm wide.

Ready when: The potatoes and carrots are cut to sizes that will roast at similar rates, and the cabbage is evenly shredded for rapid cooking.

Prep 2

Trim the mushroom stem ends and slice the mushrooms approximately 5 mm thick. Peel the onion, trim the ends and dice the usable flesh into approximately 5 mm pieces. Peel and finely grate or crush the garlic.

Ready when: The mushrooms, onion and garlic are prepared and ready for the gravy.

Prep 3

Drain the seitan if required and pat it dry. Cut or tear it into pieces approximately 25 mm across. Measure 1 tsp dried thyme, 1 tsp dried rosemary and 1 tsp reduced-salt soy sauce for the post-steaming herb coating.

Ready when: The seitan is evenly portioned and the roasting seasonings are ready, but no roasting occurs before the required steaming stage.

Prep 4

Mix the vegetable stock concentrate with approximately 300 ml hot water in the measuring jug. Keep 1 tbsp plain flour, the remaining 1 tsp soy sauce, 1/2 tsp dried thyme and 1 tsp apple cider vinegar ready for the gravy.

Ready when: The stock and remaining gravy ingredients are measured before gravy cooking begins.

Method

Step 1

Roast the potatoes and carrots

Roast vegetables
Heat source: ovenSetting: 220°CTemperature: 220°CTime: 35–40 min

Preheat the oven to 220°C. Toss the potatoes and carrots on the baking tray with 3 tsp rapeseed oil, 1/4 tsp fine salt and approximately 1/4 tsp black pepper. Spread them into a mostly single layer and roast for approximately 35-40 minutes, turning once after approximately 20 minutes.

Ready when: The potatoes are deeply golden and crisp at the edges with fluffy centres, and the carrots are tender with browned, caramelised edges.
Corrective actions
  • If: The vegetables are colouring rapidly while remaining firm inside.
    Do: Reduce the oven to 200°C and continue roasting until the centres are tender.
  • If: The vegetables remain pale after 30 minutes.
    Do: Turn them, increase spacing where possible and continue at 220°C until clear browning develops.
Step 2

Steam the seitan in the Instant Pot

Seitan
Heat source: instant_potSetting: Steam or High PressureTime: 15–18 min

Pour 250 ml water into the Instant Pot. Fit the trivet or steaming rack and arrange the seitan above the water so it is not submerged. Close the lid, set the valve to sealing and use the Steam or High Pressure programme for 5 minutes. When the programme ends, allow a 5-minute natural pressure release, then carefully release any remaining pressure before opening the lid.

Ready when: The seitan is thoroughly heated and moist from steaming, and all pressure has been safely released before the lid is opened.
Step 3

Herb-roast the steamed seitan

Seitan
Heat source: air_fryerSetting: 190°CTemperature: 190°CTime: 10–13 min

Transfer the steamed seitan to a dry bowl or plate and allow surface steam to escape for approximately 1 minute. Toss with 1 tsp rapeseed oil, 1 tsp dried thyme, 1 tsp dried rosemary, 1 tsp reduced-salt soy sauce, 1/8 tsp fine salt and approximately 1/8 tsp black pepper. Air-fry at 190°C for approximately 10-13 minutes, shaking once after approximately 6 minutes.

Ready when: The seitan is piping hot with browned, slightly crisp edges and a fragrant herb coating, while remaining moist rather than leathery.
Corrective actions
  • If: The edges darken rapidly before the seitan develops an even crust.
    Do: Reduce the air-fryer temperature to 175°C for the remaining cooking time.
  • If: The seitan remains pale after 13 minutes.
    Do: Continue air-frying for 2 minutes, checking after each minute, until distinct browned edges develop.
Step 4

Brown the onion and mushrooms

Gravy
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: medium-highTime: 8–10 min

Heat the sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add the remaining 1 tsp rapeseed oil, followed by the onion and mushrooms. Cook for approximately 8-10 minutes, stirring only occasionally at first, until the mushroom liquid has evaporated and both the mushrooms and onion have developed strong golden-brown edges. Add the garlic for the final 30-45 seconds.

Ready when: The mushrooms are browned rather than grey, the onion is fully soft and golden, and no free mushroom liquid remains in the pan.
Corrective actions
  • If: The mushrooms release liquid and begin simmering.
    Do: Maintain medium-high heat, leave the pan uncovered and stir less frequently until the liquid evaporates.
  • If: The garlic begins browning immediately.
    Do: Proceed straight to the flour and stock step to prevent scorching.
Step 5

Build and reduce the mushroom-onion gravy

Gravy
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: mediumTime: 5–7 min

Reduce the heat to medium. Sprinkle 1 tbsp plain flour over the browned mushroom-onion mixture and stir continuously for approximately 1 minute. Gradually pour in the prepared 300 ml vegetable stock while stirring to prevent lumps. Add the remaining 1 tsp soy sauce, 1/2 tsp dried thyme and remaining black pepper. Bring to a steady simmer and cook for approximately 4-6 minutes, stirring regularly. Stir in 1 tsp apple cider vinegar at the end.

Ready when: The gravy coats the back of the spoon, flows rather than sits like a paste, and tastes deeply savoury with enough acidity to avoid heaviness.
Corrective actions
  • If: The gravy becomes thicker than a pourable sauce.
    Do: Add 1 tbsp hot water and stir; repeat only if still necessary.
  • If: The gravy remains thin after 6 minutes.
    Do: Continue simmering uncovered for 1-2 minutes until it clearly coats the spoon.
  • If: Flour lumps remain visible.
    Do: Press them firmly against the side of the pan with the spatula while simmering until smooth.
Step 6

Cook the Savoy cabbage

Cabbage
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: medium-highTime: 4–5 min

Pour the finished gravy into the measuring jug and keep it warm. Return the same pan to medium-high heat. Add the shredded Savoy cabbage with 2 tbsp water and the remaining 1/8 tsp fine salt. Cover for approximately 2 minutes, then uncover and toss for another 2-3 minutes until the water has evaporated. Remove from the heat and stir through the remaining 1 tsp apple cider vinegar.

Ready when: The cabbage is tender but retains some bite, remains bright green and has no free water left in the pan.
Corrective actions
  • If: The cabbage begins catching before becoming tender.
    Do: Add 1 tbsp water and cover for another minute.
  • If: The cabbage is soft but water remains in the pan.
    Do: Uncover and cook over high heat briefly until the excess moisture evaporates.
Step 7

Finish the roast components

Final checks
Time: 1–2 min

Check the potatoes, carrots and seitan against their stated endpoints. Remove each component as soon as it is correctly browned and tender. Do not continue cooking one component merely to synchronise it with another; keep completed components warm for the few minutes required to finish plating.

Ready when: The potatoes are crisp, carrots caramelised, seitan browned and moist, cabbage tender-crisp and gravy hot and pourable.
Serving

Divide the herb-roasted seitan, crispy potatoes, roasted carrots and Savoy cabbage evenly between two warmed plates. Spoon the hot mushroom-onion gravy generously over the seitan and alongside the potatoes, leaving some crisp potato surfaces uncovered. Serve immediately.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Freezer: 1 months

Cool leftovers promptly. Refrigerate the seitan, potatoes, carrots, cabbage and gravy in sealed containers for up to 2 days, preferably keeping the gravy separate from the roasted components so they retain more texture. The seitan and gravy may be frozen separately for up to 1 month; potatoes and cabbage are best refrigerated rather than frozen.

Reheating

  • Air fryer: reheat the potatoes, carrots and seitan at approximately 180°C until piping hot and the exterior has re-crisped, checking frequently.
  • Hob: reheat the mushroom-onion gravy over medium-low heat, stirring regularly and adding 1 tbsp water if it has thickened excessively.
  • Hob: reheat the Savoy cabbage briefly in a covered pan with 1 tbsp water until piping hot, avoiding prolonged cooking that would make it soft.
  • Oven: the roasted vegetables and seitan may alternatively be reheated at approximately 190°C until piping hot.
