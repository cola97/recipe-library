Title
Harissa Tempeh, Roasted Pepper & Bulgur Salad with Lemon Yoghurt and Parsley
Description
A portable high-protein lunch salad of deeply browned harissa-coated tempeh, sweet roasted red pepper and nutty bulgur, balanced with sharp lemon yoghurt, fresh parsley and restrained seasoning. Components are cooled before packing so the salad remains fresh rather than steaming itself in transit.

Restaurant Quality Features

Tempeh is browned before harissa is added, creating genuine roasted flavour rather than simply coating pale tempeh with sauce.
Harissa, cumin and smoked paprika are briefly bloomed against the hot tempeh before being deglazed with lemon, giving a more integrated and aromatic glaze.
Pepper and onion are roasted separately at high heat so their natural sugars caramelise without steaming the tempeh.
The bulgur is seasoned with lemon, parsley and black pepper while still structurally separate, preventing bland grain beneath strongly flavoured toppings.
Thick lemon-zest yoghurt supplies cool acidity and creaminess without requiring a high-fat dressing.
Fresh parsley is divided between the bulgur and final assembly so the herb contributes both integrated flavour and a fresher finishing character.
Cooling components before packing protects the texture of the grain and prevents condensation from diluting the harissa glaze.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete lunch serving including tempeh, bulgur, vegetables, olive oil, harissa, Greek yoghurt, parsley and all seasoning.
The approximate 31 g protein per serving comes principally from tempeh and high-protein 0% Greek yoghurt, with a smaller contribution from bulgur.
The recipe is designed around the approximately 400 kcal lunch target; exact energy will vary with the nutritional composition of the chosen tempeh, yoghurt and harissa.
Using a measured small quantity of olive oil preserves browning and flavour without adding unnecessary calories.
Pepper, onion, parsley and whole-grain bulgur increase fibre, vegetable content and micronutrient variety.

Critical Success Factors

Brown the tempeh before adding harissa; adding the paste too early will inhibit browning and increase the risk of scorched spices.
Do not proceed with assembly until the bulgur has absorbed all free water and remains loose rather than wet.
Roast pepper and onion until browned at the edges rather than merely softened; this provides necessary sweetness against the acidic yoghurt and spicy harissa.
Cool all cooked ingredients before sealing the transport containers to prevent condensation and textural deterioration.
Keep the lemon yoghurt thick and separate until eating rather than mixing it through the salad several hours in advance.
Use the final reserved salt only after tasting the complete salad because supermarket harissa pastes vary significantly in salt concentration.
