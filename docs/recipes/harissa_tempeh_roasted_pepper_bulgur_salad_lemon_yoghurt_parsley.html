Harissa Tempeh, Roasted Pepper & Bulgur Salad with Lemon Yoghurt and Parsley

A portable high-protein lunch salad of deeply browned harissa-coated tempeh, sweet roasted red pepper and nutty bulgur, balanced with sharp lemon yoghurt, fresh parsley and restrained seasoning. Components are cooled before packing so the salad remains fresh rather than steaming itself in transit.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Wash and dry the red pepper. Halve it lengthwise, remove the stem, seeds and white membranes, then cut the usable flesh into approximately 25 mm pieces. Peel the red onion, trim the root and stem ends, halve it, weigh or estimate approximately 60 g usable flesh, and cut that portion into wedges approximately 8 mm wide at the thickest point. Reserve the unused onion refrigerated for another use.

Ready when: Pepper and onion are clean, dry and cut into similarly sized pieces suitable for even roasting.

Prep 2

Remove 180 g tempeh from its packaging. Pat all surfaces dry with clean kitchen paper. Cut into approximately 15 mm cubes, keeping the pieces as uniform as practical so they brown at a similar rate.

Ready when: Tempeh is dry on the surface and cut into approximately uniform 15 mm cubes.

Prep 3

Wash and dry the lemon. Finely grate approximately 2 g of the yellow outer zest without removing significant white pith. Cut the lemon and squeeze 30 ml juice through a sieve or remove seeds manually.

Ready when: Approximately 2 g fine zest and 30 ml seed-free lemon juice are prepared.

Prep 4

Wash the parsley thoroughly in cold water and dry completely. Remove and discard coarse lower stems. Retain tender upper stems and leaves, then chop into pieces approximately 3-5 mm across.

Ready when: 20 g parsley is clean, dry and evenly chopped.

Prep 5

Measure 20 g harissa paste, 0.5 tsp ground cumin, 0.5 tsp smoked paprika, 0.25 tsp initial fine salt, 0.125 tsp reserved fine salt, 0.25 tsp black pepper, 4 g olive oil, 50 g dry bulgur, 220 g 0% Greek yoghurt and 100 ml water. Keep yoghurt refrigerated until required.

Ready when: All measured ingredients are ready for immediate controlled use.

Method

Step 1

Preheat the air fryer

Roasted vegetables
Heat source: Air fryerSetting: 200°CTemperature: 200°CTime: 3–5 min

Preheat the air fryer to 200°C. Allow the appliance to reach its programmed operating temperature before loading the vegetables.

Ready when: The air fryer has completed its preheat cycle or has operated empty at 200°C for at least 3 minutes.
Corrective actions
  • If: The appliance does not indicate completion of preheating after 5 minutes.
    Do: Confirm the temperature setting is 200°C and allow an additional 2 minutes before loading.
Step 2

Hydrate the bulgur

Bulgur
Heat source: Electric kettleSetting: Boiling waterTemperature: 100°CTime: 10–15 min

Boil the measured 100 ml water in the kettle. Place 50 g dry bulgur in the heatproof mixing bowl and add approximately 0.0625 tsp of the measured initial salt. Pour the full 100 ml boiling water over the bulgur, stir once, cover the bowl with a plate or suitable lid and leave undisturbed until the grains absorb the water.

Ready when: The bulgur is tender but retains slight chew, individual grains are distinguishable and no free water remains at the base of the bowl.
Corrective actions
  • If: The bulgur remains noticeably firm after 15 minutes and the bowl is dry.
    Do: Add 15 ml freshly boiled water, cover again and reassess after 5 minutes.
  • If: Free water remains after the grains are tender.
    Do: Leave the bowl uncovered for 2-3 minutes and fluff with a fork until the excess moisture is absorbed or evaporated.
Step 3

Roast the pepper and onion

Roasted vegetables
Heat source: Air fryerSetting: 200°CTemperature: 200°CTime: 9–13 min

Combine the prepared pepper and onion with 2 g of the measured olive oil, approximately 0.0625 tsp of the initial salt and approximately 0.125 tsp black pepper. Toss until the surfaces are thinly coated. Load into the air-fryer basket in a loose single layer where practical. Roast at 200°C, shaking or turning after approximately 5 minutes.

Ready when: The pepper is tender with browned and lightly blistered edges, and the onion is softened with caramelised tips but is not blackened.
Corrective actions
  • If: The onion edges darken significantly before the pepper softens.
    Do: Remove the most browned onion pieces and continue cooking the pepper in 2-minute intervals.
  • If: The vegetables remain pale after 10 minutes.
    Do: Redistribute into a thinner layer and continue at 200°C in 2-minute intervals until browned edges develop.
Step 4

Brown the tempeh

Tempeh
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: Medium-highTime: 6–9 min

Place the frying pan over medium-high gas heat for approximately 1 minute. Add the remaining 2 g olive oil and immediately add the 180 g tempeh cubes in a single layer. Add approximately 0.0625 tsp of the initial salt. Cook while turning the cubes every 60-90 seconds so several faces contact the pan.

Ready when: The tempeh has developed visible golden-brown patches on multiple faces, feels dry rather than greasy on the exterior and releases readily from the pan when turned.
Corrective actions
  • If: The tempeh begins blackening before several faces brown.
    Do: Reduce the gas flame to medium and turn the affected cubes immediately.
  • If: The tempeh remains pale after 6 minutes.
    Do: Increase the heat slightly and leave each face undisturbed for approximately 60 seconds before turning.
Step 5

Bloom the spices and glaze the tempeh

Harissa glaze
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: Medium-lowTime: 1–2 min

Reduce the gas heat to medium-low. Add 20 g harissa paste, 0.5 tsp ground cumin and 0.5 tsp smoked paprika directly to the browned tempeh. Stir continuously for approximately 30 seconds so the spices become aromatic without scorching. Add 10 ml of the prepared lemon juice and stir, scraping the pan surface, until the liquid evaporates and the harissa forms a thin glossy coating around the tempeh.

Ready when: The tempeh is uniformly coated in a dark red-orange glaze, the spices smell aromatic rather than raw and no free liquid remains in the pan.
Corrective actions
  • If: The harissa begins sticking aggressively or darkening before the lemon juice is added.
    Do: Remove the pan from the flame, add the measured lemon juice immediately and stir before returning to low heat.
  • If: Free liquid remains after 2 minutes.
    Do: Increase heat to medium and stir continuously until the glaze clings to the tempeh with no liquid pooling.
Step 6

Fluff and season the bulgur

Bulgur
Time: 1–2 min

Uncover the hydrated bulgur. Fluff thoroughly with a fork to separate the grains. Add 10 ml prepared lemon juice, approximately half of the chopped parsley and the remaining 0.125 tsp black pepper. Fold until evenly distributed without compressing the grains.

Ready when: The bulgur is loose, evenly seasoned and lightly flecked with parsley, with no compact clumps.
Corrective actions
  • If: The bulgur is sticky or compacted.
    Do: Use a fork to lift and separate the grains repeatedly rather than stirring vigorously.
Step 7

Prepare the lemon yoghurt

Lemon yoghurt
Time: 1–2 min

Place 220 g Greek yoghurt in the small bowl. Add the prepared approximately 2 g lemon zest, the remaining 10 ml lemon juice and approximately 0.0625 tsp of the initial salt. Stir until completely homogeneous and smooth.

Ready when: The yoghurt is smooth, evenly flecked with lemon zest and tastes distinctly fresh and acidic without being sharply sour.
Corrective actions
  • If: The yoghurt appears unusually stiff.
    Do: Stir vigorously for approximately 20 seconds; do not dilute unless required because the yoghurt must remain thick enough for transport.
Step 8

Cool the cooked components

Cooling
Time: 10–15 min

Transfer the roasted pepper and onion and the harissa tempeh to separate areas of a large clean plate or clean bowls so steam can escape. Leave the cooked components uncovered until they are no longer visibly steaming and are approximately room temperature. Keep the lemon yoghurt refrigerated during this cooling period.

Ready when: Tempeh, vegetables and bulgur are no longer emitting steam and feel only slightly warm or neutral to the touch before packing.
Corrective actions
  • If: The components remain noticeably hot after 15 minutes.
    Do: Spread them into thinner layers and allow an additional 5 minutes cooling before packing.
Step 9

Combine and balance the salad

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Combine the cooled bulgur, roasted pepper, roasted onion and harissa tempeh in the mixing bowl. Add the remaining chopped parsley and fold gently until evenly distributed. Taste one representative mouthful containing tempeh, grain and vegetable. If the mixture tastes under-seasoned after accounting for the salty harissa, add up to the reserved 0.125 tsp fine salt in 0.0625 tsp increments, folding thoroughly after each addition.

Ready when: The salad is evenly mixed, the tempeh retains its glaze, the grains remain loose and the flavour is balanced between salt, chilli, acidity and roasted sweetness.
Corrective actions
  • If: The salad tastes excessively sharp.
    Do: Do not add further lemon or salt; serve with the specified yoghurt, which will soften the acidity.
  • If: The salad tastes under-seasoned.
    Do: Use the reserved salt incrementally as specified, stopping as soon as the flavour becomes defined.
Step 10

Portion for transport

Packing
Time: 3–5 min

Place the two lunch containers on the kitchen scales individually and divide the bulgur-tempeh salad approximately equally between them. Divide the lemon yoghurt equally, approximately 110 g per serving. Place the yoghurt in a distinct mound or separate small internal compartment where available rather than fully stirring it into the salad. Secure the lids immediately and refrigerate.

Ready when: Both containers hold approximately equal portions, the yoghurt remains visually distinct from the harissa salad and the lids are fully sealed.
Corrective actions
  • If: One container is visibly heavier than the other.
    Do: Transfer small amounts of salad between the containers using the scales until portions are approximately equal.
  • If: The salad is still warm enough to create visible condensation after sealing.
    Do: Reopen the containers and allow further cooling before resealing and refrigerating.
Serving

Serve one complete portion per person. Immediately before eating, spoon the lemon yoghurt through the salad in broad streaks rather than fully homogenising it, preserving contrast between the spicy harissa tempeh, sweet roasted vegetables, nutty bulgur, cool yoghurt and fresh parsley.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Cool all cooked components before packing. Refrigerate the finished lunch containers at 5°C or below and consume within 2 days. For transport to work or on a car journey, carry in an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack when continuous refrigeration is not available. Keep the container closed until eating. The salad is intended to be eaten chilled or at cool room temperature.
