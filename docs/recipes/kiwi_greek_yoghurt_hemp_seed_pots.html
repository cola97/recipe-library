Description

Storage Instructions
Store the covered pots continuously at 5°C or below and consume within 24 hours for best texture. Kiwi contains enzymes that can gradually alter dairy texture during prolonged storage, so these pots are best assembled on the day they are eaten. For maximum hemp-seed crunch, the cooled toasted seeds may be kept separately in a small dry container and scattered over immediately before serving.

Restaurant Quality Features

Hemp seeds are lightly toasted to develop a more pronounced nutty aroma and textural contrast than raw seeds.
Fresh lime zest provides aromatic citrus character while a controlled quantity of juice sharpens both yoghurt and kiwi.
Vanilla and a restrained amount of honey round the acidity without making the snack excessively sweet.
A very small measured amount of salt enhances the yoghurt, honey and citrus flavours without becoming perceptibly salty.
Kiwi is cut into identifiable pieces and folded only minimally so the finished pots retain fresh fruit texture.
The toasted seeds are cooled completely before contact with the yoghurt, helping preserve their texture.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete evening snack pot including Greek yoghurt, kiwi, hemp seeds, honey, lime, vanilla and salt.
The recipe is designed around the approximately 200 kcal evening-snack target.
The general 30 g protein requirement does not apply to evening snacks under the specified meal-plan rules; approximately 17 g protein per serving is therefore compliant.
Greek yoghurt provides most of the protein while hemp seeds contribute additional protein and predominantly unsaturated fat.
Kiwi provides fruit, fibre and vitamin C while allowing added honey to remain limited.
Nutrition values are estimates and will vary with kiwi size and the exact Greek yoghurt and hemp seed products used.
The recipe follows the supplied structured-recipe and evening-snack requirements.

Critical Success Factors

Toast hemp seeds only over low heat and remove them as soon as they become aromatic because their small size makes them easy to scorch.
Cool the toasted hemp seeds completely before adding them to the cold yoghurt.
Use ripe but structurally firm kiwi so the fruit remains in distinct pieces after cutting and chilling.
Do not assemble substantially more than 24 hours ahead because kiwi enzymes can progressively alter dairy texture.
Keep the completed pots continuously refrigerated until serving.
