Carrot & Cucumber Batons with Edamame Hummus

Recipe info

A portable afternoon snack of crisp carrot and cucumber batons with a bright, savoury edamame hummus flavoured with lemon, tahini, garlic and cumin. The hummus is blended until smooth but substantial, while the vegetables are kept dry and separate until eating to preserve crunch.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Wash the 2 carrots under cold running water and dry them. Peel only if the outer surface is tough or damaged. Trim and discard both ends. Cut the usable flesh into batons approximately 10 mm x 10 mm x 70 mm.

Ready when: Approximately 160 g carrot is cut into firm, evenly sized batons suitable for dipping.

Prep 2

Wash and dry the whole cucumber. Cut approximately half of it to provide about 200 g. Trim the cut end if required. Cut the usable portion into batons approximately 10 mm x 10 mm x 70 mm. If the seed core is exceptionally soft and watery, trim away only the softest central material. Pat all cut surfaces dry with clean kitchen paper.

Ready when: Approximately 200 g cucumber is cut into crisp, dry batons with minimal free surface moisture.

Prep 3

Wash and dry the lemon. Finely grate approximately 1 g yellow zest without removing significant white pith. Cut the lemon and squeeze 20 ml juice, removing all seeds. Separate 1 small garlic clove from the bulb, peel it, trim the root end and grate it finely.

Ready when: Approximately 1 g lemon zest, 20 ml seed-free lemon juice and 1 finely grated garlic clove are ready for blending.

Prep 4

Weigh 160 g frozen shelled edamame, 12 g tahini and 4 g olive oil. Measure 0.25 tsp ground cumin, 0.1875 tsp initial fine salt, 0.0625 tsp reserved fine salt, 0.125 tsp black pepper and 30 ml cold water.

Ready when: All hummus ingredients are accurately measured and ready for controlled addition.

Method

Step 1

Cook the edamame

Edamame
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: High to medium-highTemperature: 100°CTime: 3–5 min

Boil sufficient water in the electric kettle to cover the 160 g frozen edamame by at least 30 mm in the saucepan. Pour the boiling water into the saucepan, place over high gas heat and add the frozen edamame. Return the water to a full boil, then reduce the flame sufficiently to maintain active boiling without overflow.

Ready when: The edamame is fully hot, bright green and tender when bitten but retains slight firmness rather than becoming mushy.
Corrective actions
  • If: The water threatens to boil over.
    Do: Reduce the gas flame immediately while maintaining visible boiling.
  • If: The beans remain hard or chalky after 5 minutes.
    Do: Continue boiling in 1-minute intervals until the stated tender-but-firm endpoint is reached.
Step 2

Prepare and dry the batons

Vegetables
Time: 5–8 min

Complete the prepared carrot and cucumber baton cuts as specified in mise en place. Inspect for irregularly thick pieces and trim them so most batons remain approximately 10 mm thick. Pat the cucumber surfaces dry before packing.

Ready when: The vegetable batons are evenly sized, crisp and externally dry, with no pooled cucumber liquid.
Corrective actions
  • If: Cucumber pieces release significant moisture after cutting.
    Do: Spread them on clean kitchen paper and blot thoroughly before packing.
Step 3

Drain and cool the edamame

Edamame
Time: 2–4 min

Drain the cooked edamame immediately through the colander. Rinse under cold running water while agitating the beans gently until they are no longer hot. Shake the colander firmly several times and leave it to drain for approximately 2 minutes so excess water is removed.

Ready when: The edamame is cool, bright green and visibly free of standing or dripping water.
Corrective actions
  • If: Water continues dripping heavily from the colander after 2 minutes.
    Do: Shake again and leave to drain for an additional 1-2 minutes before blending.
Step 4

Blend the hummus base

Edamame hummus
Time: 1–2 min

Add the drained 160 g cooked edamame to the blender with 12 g tahini, 20 ml lemon juice, approximately 1 g lemon zest, the finely grated garlic, 4 g olive oil, 0.25 tsp cumin, 0.1875 tsp fine salt, 0.125 tsp black pepper and 15 ml of the measured cold water. Blend for approximately 30 seconds, stop and scrape down the sides, then blend again until the mixture becomes mostly smooth.

Ready when: The hummus is pale green, cohesive and mostly smooth with no whole edamame remaining, but retains enough body to hold a soft mound on a spoon.
Corrective actions
  • If: The blender blades spin without circulating the mixture or the hummus remains excessively stiff.
    Do: Add the remaining cold water in 5 ml increments, blending after each addition, stopping as soon as the mixture circulates and reaches the specified thick dipping consistency.
  • If: The mixture becomes thinner than intended.
    Do: Stop adding water immediately and allow the hummus to stand refrigerated for 10 minutes; do not add unlisted ingredients to thicken it.
Step 5

Check seasoning and texture

Edamame hummus
Time: 1–2 min

Taste a small amount of hummus after blending. Confirm that lemon acidity, sesame richness, garlic, cumin and salt are individually perceptible but balanced. If the mixture tastes under-seasoned, add up to the reserved 0.0625 tsp fine salt and blend for approximately 10 seconds.

Ready when: The hummus tastes savoury, lemon-bright and clearly seasoned without excessive salt, and retains a thick dipping consistency.
Corrective actions
  • If: The hummus tastes adequately seasoned before the reserved salt is used.
    Do: Do not add the reserved salt.
  • If: The hummus appears overly thick after standing.
    Do: Blend in any remaining measured water in 5 ml increments until the acceptance texture is restored.
Step 6

Portion the snack containers

Packing
Time: 3–5 min

Divide the finished edamame hummus equally between the 2 snack containers using the kitchen scales. Divide the carrot and cucumber batons approximately equally between the containers. Keep the vegetable batons physically separate from the hummus using an internal divider or separate small pot where available. Secure all lids fully and refrigerate immediately.

Ready when: Each serving contains approximately half of the hummus and half of the prepared vegetables, the vegetables remain dry and separate from the dip, and all lids are secure for transport.
Corrective actions
  • If: One hummus portion is visibly larger than the other.
    Do: Use the kitchen scales to transfer small amounts until both portions are approximately equal.
  • If: Cucumber moisture has collected in a container before sealing.
    Do: Remove the cucumber, blot dry again and dry the container before repacking.
Serving

Serve one complete snack container per person. Keep the hummus and vegetable batons separate until eating, then dip the carrot and cucumber directly into the edamame hummus to preserve the contrast between crisp vegetables and creamy dip.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Refrigerate at 5°C or below and consume within 2 days. For best texture, keep the carrot and cucumber batons separate from the hummus until eating. The snack is suitable for transport to work or on a car journey; if continuous refrigeration is unavailable, carry it in an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack and keep it chilled until consumption.
