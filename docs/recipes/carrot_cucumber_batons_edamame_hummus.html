Title
Carrot & Cucumber Batons with Edamame Hummus
Description
A portable afternoon snack of crisp carrot and cucumber batons with a bright, savoury edamame hummus flavoured with lemon, tahini, garlic and cumin. The hummus is blended until smooth but substantial, while the vegetables are kept dry and separate until eating to preserve crunch.

Mise En Place

Item 1
Prep Id
PREP_01

Ingredient Ids

carrots
Instruction
Wash the 2 carrots under cold running water and dry them. Peel only if the outer surface is tough or damaged. Trim and discard both ends. Cut the usable flesh into batons approximately 10 mm x 10 mm x 70 mm.
Endpoint
Approximately 160 g carrot is cut into firm, evenly sized batons suitable for dipping.
Item 2
Prep Id
PREP_02

Ingredient Ids

cucumber
Instruction
Wash and dry the whole cucumber. Cut approximately half of it to provide about 200 g. Trim the cut end if required. Cut the usable portion into batons approximately 10 mm x 10 mm x 70 mm. If the seed core is exceptionally soft and watery, trim away only the softest central material. Pat all cut surfaces dry with clean kitchen paper.
Endpoint
Approximately 200 g cucumber is cut into crisp, dry batons with minimal free surface moisture.
Item 3
Prep Id
PREP_03

Ingredient Ids

lemon
garlic
Instruction
Wash and dry the lemon. Finely grate approximately 1 g yellow zest without removing significant white pith. Cut the lemon and squeeze 20 ml juice, removing all seeds. Separate 1 small garlic clove from the bulb, peel it, trim the root end and grate it finely.
Endpoint
Approximately 1 g lemon zest, 20 ml seed-free lemon juice and 1 finely grated garlic clove are ready for blending.

All hummus ingredients are accurately measured and ready for controlled addition.

Restaurant Quality Features

Edamame replaces conventional chickpeas to give the dip a fresher green flavour and naturally higher protein density.
Lemon juice and zest provide both acidity and aromatic freshness rather than relying on excess oil for flavour.
Tahini supplies sesame depth and creaminess while remaining deliberately restrained to keep the snack near the 200 kcal target.
Garlic and cumin provide savoury aromatic complexity despite the recipe requiring no frying or roasting stage.
Cold water is added incrementally during blending, allowing the hummus to become smooth without becoming runny.
Drying the cucumber thoroughly and packing the vegetables separately protects their crunch during transport.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete snack serving including vegetables, edamame hummus, tahini, olive oil and all seasonings.
This recipe is designed around the approximately 200 kcal afternoon-snack target.
The general 30 g protein target does not apply to afternoon snacks under the specified meal-plan rules; approximately 11 g protein per serving is therefore compliant.
Edamame provides the majority of the protein as well as fibre and unsaturated fat.
Carrot and cucumber contribute additional fibre, fluid volume and micronutrients while keeping energy density modest.
Nutrition values are estimates and will vary with exact vegetable size and supermarket products.

Critical Success Factors

Drain the cooked edamame thoroughly before blending; excess water will dilute flavour and make the hummus difficult to pack.
Add blending water incrementally rather than all at once so the hummus remains thick enough for dipping.
Keep cucumber surfaces dry and physically separate from the hummus until eating to prevent watery dilution.
Do not automatically add the reserved salt; taste the hummus first because tahini and individual ingredient characteristics vary.
Keep the completed snack chilled during storage and transport.
