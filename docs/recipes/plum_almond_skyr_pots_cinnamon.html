Title
Plum & Almond Skyr Pots with Cinnamon
Description
Portable morning snack pots of thick plain skyr layered with lightly macerated fresh plum and finished with cinnamon-toasted almonds. Lemon and a restrained amount of honey sharpen the fruit while the toasted nuts provide crisp contrast against the cool dairy.

Restaurant Quality Features

Plums are briefly macerated with lemon, honey and cinnamon so their natural juices create a fresh fruit sauce without cooking the fruit into a compote.
Lemon zest and juice sharpen ripe plum flavour and balance the creamy skyr.
Almonds are toasted before seasoning, developing deeper nutty aromas and a crisp texture.
Cinnamon is divided between the plum and almond components, creating integrated spice flavour rather than a dusty surface garnish.
A very small measured quantity of salt heightens the toasted almond flavour without making the snack taste savoury.
Vanilla is incorporated into the skyr to provide aromatic sweetness without requiring additional sugar.
Distinct layers preserve textural contrast between creamy skyr, juicy fruit and crisp nuts.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete morning snack pot including skyr, plum, almonds, honey, lemon, cinnamon, vanilla and salt.
The recipe is designed around the approximately 200 kcal morning-snack target.
The general 30 g protein requirement does not apply to morning snacks under the specified meal-plan rules; approximately 15 g protein per serving is therefore compliant.
Skyr provides most of the protein, while almonds contribute additional protein, fibre and predominantly unsaturated fat.
Fresh plum contributes fruit, fibre and natural sweetness, allowing added honey to remain limited.
Nutrition values are estimates and will vary with plum size and the exact skyr and almond products selected.
The recipe follows the supplied structured-recipe and morning-snack requirements.

Critical Success Factors

Use ripe but still firm plums so the fruit releases juice during maceration without collapsing into puree.
Do not over-toast the almonds; remove them from direct heat as soon as they become aromatic and lightly golden.
Cool toasted almonds completely before sealing the pots to prevent condensation and loss of crunch.
Keep the added honey restrained because ripe plum and vanilla already contribute perceived sweetness.
Maintain refrigeration during storage and use an insulated bag with an ice pack when transporting the dairy-based snack for an extended period.
