Baked Plum & Quark Pots with Cinnamon and Walnuts

Recipe info

Warm cinnamon-baked plums spooned over cool creamy quark with crunchy walnuts, creating a simple sweet-tart evening snack with strong temperature and texture contrast.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Halve the washed plums around the stones, twist the halves apart and discard the stones. Cut each half into 3 wedges, retaining the edible skins.

Ready when: The plums are divided into 12 similarly sized wedges that will soften evenly during baking.

Prep 2

Weigh 16 g walnuts and roughly chop them into coarse pieces approximately 5-8 mm across.

Ready when: The walnuts remain in substantial crunchy pieces rather than fine crumbs.

Method

Step 1

Season the plums

Plums
Time: 1–2 min

Place the plum wedges in the air-fryer-safe dish. Add 1 tsp runny honey and 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon, then turn the wedges gently until their cut surfaces are lightly coated.

Ready when: The plum wedges are evenly cinnamon-coated with a thin sheen of honey and remain intact.
Corrective actions
  • If: The plums are extremely ripe and begin breaking apart during mixing.
    Do: Stop turning them and use a spoon to distribute the honey and cinnamon over their surfaces instead.
Step 2

Bake until tender and glossy

Plums
Heat source: air_fryerSetting: 190°CTemperature: 190°CTime: 9–11 min

Place the dish in the air fryer and bake at 190°C for approximately 9-11 minutes. Check once after approximately 8 minutes and gently turn the wedges if one side is colouring significantly faster.

Ready when: The plums are fully tender when pierced with the tip of a knife, their edges are beginning to caramelise, and the released juices have become glossy and lightly syrupy rather than watery.
Corrective actions
  • If: The plum edges darken before the flesh becomes tender.
    Do: Reduce the air-fryer temperature to 175°C and continue until the wedges yield easily to the knife.
  • If: The plums are tender but the juices remain very watery.
    Do: Continue baking for 1-2 minutes, checking every minute, until the juices visibly thicken.
Step 3

Portion the quark

Assembly
Time: 1–2 min

While the plums bake, divide the 320 g quark evenly between the two serving pots, giving approximately 160 g per serving. Keep refrigerated if the plums still have several minutes to cook.

Ready when: Each pot contains an even portion of cold, thick quark.
Step 4

Rest the baked plums briefly

Assembly
Time: 2 min

Remove the baked plums from the air fryer and leave them in their dish for approximately 2 minutes so the bubbling juices settle and the fruit cools slightly before meeting the cold quark.

Ready when: The plums remain distinctly warm but are no longer aggressively steaming, and the juices coat the fruit rather than running like water.
Step 5

Finish the pots

Assembly
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the warm baked plum wedges evenly over the cold quark, spooning the cinnamon-plum juices over the fruit. Scatter half of the chopped walnuts over each pot immediately before serving.

Ready when: Each serving has distinct layers of cool creamy quark, warm glossy plum and crisp walnuts, with no excessive liquid pooling in the bottom.
Corrective actions
  • If: The plum dish contains more thin liquid than is desirable for the pots.
    Do: Spoon the fruit onto the quark first and add only enough of the juices to lightly glaze the surface.
Serving

Serve immediately for the strongest contrast between cold quark, warm cinnamon-baked plum and crunchy walnuts. Stir lightly while eating rather than fully mixing the pot before serving.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

For make-ahead use, cool the baked plums completely before refrigerating them separately from the quark. Store both in sealed containers for up to 2 days. Keep the walnuts dry and separate until immediately before eating. The snack may be served chilled if prepared ahead; warming the plums is optional rather than necessary.

Reheating

  • Air fryer: if warm plums are preferred, place only the baked plum portion in a suitable heatproof dish and heat at approximately 160°C for 2-3 minutes until gently warmed, then spoon over cold quark.
  • Hob: place only the baked plums and their juices in a small saucepan over low heat for 1-2 minutes until gently warmed.
