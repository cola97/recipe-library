Spinach, Tomato & Cottage Cheese Egg Muffins with Seeded Wholemeal Toast

Recipe info

Tender baked egg muffins with spinach, tomato and cottage cheese, seasoned with Dijon, smoked paprika and oregano, served with crisp seeded wholemeal toast for a practical high-protein weekday breakfast.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Roughly chop the dry spinach into pieces approximately 15-20 mm across. Quarter the cherry tomatoes and place the pieces cut-side down briefly on clean kitchen paper or a clean towel to remove obvious surface juice.

Ready when: The spinach is evenly chopped and the tomatoes are quartered without excess free liquid.

Prep 2

Crack the 2 eggs into the mixing bowl. Add 150 g liquid egg whites, 180 g cottage cheese, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp dried oregano, 1/4 tsp fine salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper.

Ready when: All egg-muffin base ingredients are measured together and ready to mix.

Method

Step 1

Preheat and grease the muffin tin

Preparation
Heat source: ovenSetting: 200°CTemperature: 200°CTime: 4–6 min

Preheat the oven to 200°C. Use the 1 tsp olive oil to coat the base and lower sides of all 6 muffin holes in a thin, even film.

Ready when: The oven is fully preheated and the muffin tin has a continuous light oil coating with no large pools.
Step 2

Mix the filling

Egg mixture
Time: 1–2 min

Whisk the eggs, egg whites, cottage cheese and seasonings for approximately 30-45 seconds until the yolks are completely broken and the cottage cheese is evenly distributed but still visibly curded. Fold in the chopped spinach and quartered tomatoes.

Ready when: The mixture is evenly combined, with vegetables distributed throughout and no separate pools of egg white.
Corrective actions
  • If: A large amount of tomato juice collects in the mixture.
    Do: Spoon off the obvious free liquid before filling the muffin tin so the muffins bake rather than steam.
Step 3

Fill and bake the egg muffins

Egg muffins
Heat source: ovenSetting: 200°CTemperature: 200°CTime: 14–16 min

Divide the egg mixture evenly between the 6 prepared muffin holes. Bake at 200°C for approximately 14-16 minutes.

Ready when: The muffins are puffed and lightly golden at the edges, the centres are fully set rather than liquid, and a gentle press on the centre springs back without releasing raw egg.
Corrective actions
  • If: The tops brown strongly while the centres still appear wet.
    Do: Reduce the oven to 180°C and continue baking for 2-3 minutes until the centres are set.
  • If: The muffins remain pale and visibly soft after 16 minutes.
    Do: Continue baking in 1-minute increments until the centres pass the stated set test.
Step 4

Toast the seeded wholemeal bread

Toast
Heat source: toasterSetting: mediumTime: 2–4 min

During the final few minutes of baking, toast the 4 slices of seeded wholemeal bread until the surfaces are crisp and golden while the centres remain tender.

Ready when: The bread is evenly toasted with crisp surfaces and no burnt seed or crust areas.
Corrective actions
  • If: The exposed seeds begin darkening rapidly.
    Do: Stop the toaster early rather than allowing the seed topping to burn.
Step 5

Rest and release the muffins

Finishing
Time: 2 min

Remove the muffin tin from the oven and leave the muffins to stand for approximately 2 minutes. Run a blunt knife or thin spatula around the edges only where necessary, then lift the muffins out carefully.

Ready when: The muffins release intact, remain tender and hold their shape without obvious liquid escaping.
Corrective actions
  • If: A muffin sticks firmly to the tin.
    Do: Leave it for another minute, then loosen the full circumference before attempting to lift it.
Serving

Serve 3 warm spinach, tomato and cottage-cheese egg muffins per person with 2 slices of crisp seeded wholemeal toast. Eat immediately while the muffin centres remain tender and the toast is still crisp.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Freezer: 1 months

Cool leftover egg muffins promptly and refrigerate in a sealed container for up to 2 days. Keep bread separate and toast it fresh when possible. Egg muffins may be frozen for up to 1 month once completely cool, with parchment between layers.

Reheating

  • Air fryer: reheat egg muffins at approximately 160°C for 3-5 minutes until piping hot throughout, checking early to avoid drying them.
  • Oven: reheat at approximately 170°C until piping hot throughout.
  • Toast fresh seeded wholemeal bread separately immediately before serving.
