Raspberry-Lemon Protein Pancakes with Skyr and Pistachios

Mise en place

Prep 1

Finely grate the zest from approximately half of the lemon. Halve the lemon and squeeze the juice. Stir approximately 1 tsp lemon juice into 180 g of the skyr reserved for serving; keep the remaining juice available for final adjustment.

Ready when: The serving skyr is smooth with a clean lemon acidity but remains thick enough to spoon.

Prep 2

Roughly chop the pistachios. Inspect the raspberries, rinse gently if necessary and dry thoroughly so they do not water down the finished pancakes.

Ready when: The pistachios are in coarse crunchy pieces and the raspberries are clean and dry.

Prep 3

Measure 50 g plain flour, 1 tsp baking powder and 1/8 tsp fine salt into the mixing bowl and whisk briefly to distribute the raising agent evenly.

Ready when: The dry ingredients are evenly combined with no visible pockets of baking powder.

Method

Step 1

Mix the pancake batter

Batter
Time: 2–3 min

Add 200 g liquid egg whites, 1 medium egg, the remaining 60 g skyr, 1 tsp vanilla extract and the prepared lemon zest to the dry ingredients. Whisk just until the batter is smooth and no dry flour remains. Do not continue whisking once combined.

Ready when: The batter is smooth, thick but pourable, and falls from the whisk in a broad ribbon.
Corrective actions
  • If: The batter is so thick that it does not spread at all when spooned.
    Do: Mix in 1 tsp lemon juice or cold water, then reassess before adding more.
  • If: The batter appears thin and watery.
    Do: Leave it undisturbed for 2 minutes so the flour hydrates before deciding whether any correction is needed.
Step 2

Heat the pan

Cooking
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: mediumTime: 1–2 min

Place the frying pan over medium heat for approximately 1-2 minutes. Add approximately 1/2 tsp rapeseed oil and spread it into a very thin film over the cooking surface.

Ready when: The pan is evenly hot and the oil flows readily across the surface without smoking.
Corrective actions
  • If: The oil begins smoking.
    Do: Remove the pan from the heat for approximately 30 seconds and reduce the burner before adding batter.
Step 3

Cook the first batch

Cooking
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: mediumTime: 3–5 min

Spoon 3 small pancakes into the pan, using approximately one-sixth of the batter for each. Leave space between them. Cook over medium heat for approximately 2-3 minutes, until the edges look set, the upper surfaces show bubbles and the undersides are evenly golden. Turn once and cook for a further 1-2 minutes, until the second side is golden and the centres are just set.

Ready when: The pancakes are golden on both sides, spring lightly when pressed and have no wet batter visible at the centre.
Corrective actions
  • If: The undersides darken before bubbles appear and the edges set.
    Do: Reduce the heat before continuing; the pan is too hot.
  • If: The pancakes remain pale after 3 minutes and spread excessively.
    Do: Increase the heat slightly and allow the pan to recover before turning.
  • If: The pancakes tear when turned.
    Do: Wait another 30-45 seconds until the upper surface is more firmly set before trying again.
Step 4

Cook the second batch

Cooking
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: mediumTime: 3–5 min

Add the remaining rapeseed oil only if the pan surface is dry. Cook the remaining batter as 3 pancakes using the same method and endpoint as the first batch.

Ready when: All 6 pancakes are evenly golden, tender and fully set through the centre without becoming dry.
Corrective actions
  • If: The second batch colours more rapidly than the first.
    Do: Reduce the heat slightly because the pan has accumulated additional heat.
Step 5

Balance the lemon skyr

Finishing
Time: 1 min

Taste the lemon skyr. If it needs more brightness, stir in up to another 1 tsp lemon juice. Keep the texture thick enough to sit on the pancakes rather than run off them.

Ready when: The skyr tastes distinctly lemony and fresh without becoming sharply sour or watery.
Serving

Place 3 warm pancakes on each plate. Spoon half of the lemon skyr alongside or over each stack, divide the raspberries and chopped pistachios evenly between the plates, then drizzle each serving with half of the 1 tbsp maple syrup. Serve immediately so the pancakes remain warm against the cool skyr and raspberries.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Freezer: 1 months

Cool leftover pancakes promptly and refrigerate in a sealed container for up to 2 days, keeping the skyr, raspberries, pistachios and maple syrup separate. Pancakes may be frozen with parchment between layers for up to 1 month. Refrigerate opened skyr according to its package instructions.

Reheating

  • Toaster: reheat chilled pancakes briefly on a moderate setting until hot throughout, checking early to prevent excessive browning.
  • Frying pan: warm over low-medium heat for approximately 1-2 minutes per side until hot throughout.
  • Air fryer: heat at approximately 160°C for a few minutes until hot, checking frequently because the pancakes can dry quickly.
