Miso-Ginger Tofu, Broccoli & Brown Basmati Stir-Fry with Sesame and Edamame

Recipe info

A high-protein dinner of crisp-edged tofu, broccoli and edamame tossed through a glossy miso-ginger sauce and served with nutty brown basmati rice. High wok heat develops browning before the sauce is added, while rice vinegar, ginger and toasted sesame keep the finished dish savoury, aromatic and balanced rather than heavy.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Weigh 80 g dry brown basmati rice. Place it in the fine sieve and rinse under cold running water while agitating the grains with clean fingers until the runoff is substantially clearer. Shake the sieve thoroughly to remove excess water.

Ready when: The rice is thoroughly rinsed and no longer releases heavily cloudy water.

Prep 2

Drain 320 g extra-firm tofu. Pat all surfaces repeatedly with clean kitchen paper until little visible moisture transfers. Cut the tofu into approximately 20 mm cubes. Weigh 6 g of the cornflour separately for coating.

Ready when: The tofu is surface-dry, evenly cubed and the coating cornflour is separately measured.

Prep 3

Wash and dry the broccoli. Separate the crown into florets approximately 30-40 mm across. Trim the stem base. Peel away any visibly tough outer stem skin, then cut the tender stem flesh into pieces approximately 5 mm thick and 40 mm long. Weigh or estimate approximately 300 g usable broccoli in total.

Ready when: Approximately 300 g broccoli is prepared into similarly quick-cooking florets and stem pieces.

Prep 4

Wash the ginger and peel only if the skin is thick or dry. Finely grate 20 g usable ginger. Separate 2 garlic cloves from the bulb, peel them, trim the root ends and finely grate.

Ready when: The ginger and garlic are finely grated and ready to disperse rapidly through the sauce.

Prep 5

Measure 20 g white miso, 20 ml reduced-salt soy sauce, 20 ml rice vinegar, 8 g honey, 80 ml water, the remaining 4 g cornflour, 4 g toasted sesame oil, the prepared 20 g grated ginger and the prepared grated garlic. Add all of these ingredients to the small bowl and whisk until the miso and cornflour are fully dispersed with no visible lumps.

Ready when: The sauce is homogeneous, pourable and free from visible miso or cornflour lumps.

Prep 6

Weigh 140 g frozen shelled edamame, 10 g sesame seeds and 4 g rapeseed oil into separate measured quantities. Keep the edamame frozen until the stir-fry stage.

Ready when: Edamame, sesame seeds and high-heat cooking oil are accurately measured and immediately available.

Method

Step 1

Cook the brown basmati rice

Brown basmati rice
Heat source: Rice cookerSetting: Brown rice programmeTime: 30–45 min

Transfer the rinsed 80 g brown basmati rice to the rice cooker. Add the quantity of water specified by the rice cooker's brown-rice markings or manufacturer instructions for this amount of rice. Select the brown-rice programme where available and start the cooking cycle. Keep the lid closed throughout cooking.

Ready when: The rice cooker has completed its cycle, the grains are tender with a slight chew, no hard core remains and there is no free water at the base.
Corrective actions
  • If: The rice remains noticeably firm after the rice cooker completes and no free water remains.
    Do: Add 30 ml hot water, close the lid and continue using the cook or keep-warm function for 5-10 minutes before reassessing.
  • If: The grains are cooked but visibly wet.
    Do: Leave the rice cooker open on keep-warm for several minutes and gently fluff once excess moisture has evaporated.
Step 2

Toast the sesame seeds

Sesame garnish
Heat source: Wok burnerSetting: LowTime: 2–4 min

Place the dry wok over low heat on the wok burner. Add 10 g sesame seeds. Stir continuously so the seeds move across the hot surface rather than remaining stationary.

Ready when: The sesame seeds are lightly golden in places and smell distinctly nutty without smoke or blackened seeds.
Corrective actions
  • If: The seeds darken rapidly or begin popping aggressively.
    Do: Remove the wok from the heat immediately and transfer the seeds to a cool plate or bowl.
  • If: No aroma develops after 4 minutes.
    Do: Increase the heat slightly and continue stirring continuously in 20-second intervals.
Step 3

Coat the tofu

Tofu
Time: 1–2 min

Place the prepared tofu cubes in the mixing bowl. Add the separately measured 6 g cornflour. Toss gently but thoroughly until all tofu surfaces have a very thin, dry coating and no loose pockets of cornflour remain in the bottom of the bowl.

Ready when: Each tofu cube has a thin, even powdery coating without thick white clumps.
Corrective actions
  • If: Cornflour accumulates in thick patches.
    Do: Continue tossing gently and rub excessive powder from affected cubes onto uncoated surfaces.
Step 4

Sear the tofu

Tofu
Heat source: Wok burnerSetting: HighTime: 6–9 min

Wipe any remaining sesame fragments from the wok if necessary. Heat the wok over high heat until a droplet of water evaporates almost immediately on contact. Add 4 g rapeseed oil and rotate the wok to coat the cooking surface. Immediately add the coated tofu in as close to a single layer as practical. Leave undisturbed for approximately 60-90 seconds before turning, then continue turning at intervals so multiple faces brown.

Ready when: The tofu has crisp golden-brown patches on multiple faces, remains intact and releases cleanly from the wok when moved.
Corrective actions
  • If: The tofu blackens before becoming evenly golden.
    Do: Reduce the burner to medium-high and turn the affected pieces immediately.
  • If: The tofu sticks strongly when first turned.
    Do: Leave that face undisturbed for an additional 20-30 seconds; properly browned tofu should release more readily.
  • If: The wok is crowded and liquid begins pooling.
    Do: Remove approximately half the tofu and brown in two batches rather than allowing the tofu to steam.
Step 5

Remove and hold the browned tofu

Tofu
Time: 1–2 min

Transfer the browned tofu from the wok to a clean plate or the cleaned mixing bowl. Leave uncovered while the vegetables cook so surface steam can escape and the crust remains as dry as practical.

Ready when: The wok is empty and ready for the vegetable stage, and the browned tofu is held without being covered.
Corrective actions
  • If: A significant layer of burnt cornflour remains in the wok.
    Do: Allow the wok to cool briefly, wipe out the burnt residue carefully, then reheat before proceeding.
Step 6

Stir-fry the broccoli

Vegetables
Heat source: Wok burnerSetting: HighTime: 3–5 min

Return the empty wok to high heat. Add the prepared approximately 300 g broccoli. Stir-fry continuously for approximately 2 minutes, allowing the cut surfaces to contact the hot wok between tosses. Add 30 ml of the measured sauce water only if the broccoli begins scorching before softening, then allow the added water to evaporate completely.

Ready when: The broccoli is vivid green, tender enough to bite cleanly but still crisp, with scattered browned or blistered surfaces and no standing water.
Corrective actions
  • If: The broccoli begins blackening while still raw inside.
    Do: Reduce heat to medium-high, add up to 30 ml water as specified and cover or toss rapidly until the thickest pieces begin to soften.
  • If: The broccoli becomes soft without developing browning.
    Do: Increase the heat and cook uncovered until surface moisture evaporates; stop once light browning develops.
Step 7

Heat the edamame

Vegetables
Heat source: Wok burnerSetting: Medium-highTime: 2–4 min

Add the 140 g frozen shelled edamame directly to the broccoli. Continue stir-frying over medium-high heat, tossing frequently as the frozen beans thaw and release moisture.

Ready when: The edamame is fully hot and bright green, the broccoli remains crisp-tender and no visible water remains in the wok.
Corrective actions
  • If: Water pools in the wok as the edamame thaws.
    Do: Increase the burner to high and toss continuously until the water has completely evaporated before proceeding.
  • If: The broccoli begins becoming overly soft before the edamame is hot.
    Do: Temporarily remove the broccoli and continue heating the edamame alone until fully hot.
Step 8

Recombine and glaze the stir-fry

Miso-ginger sauce
Heat source: Wok burnerSetting: MediumTime: 1–3 min

Stir the prepared sauce thoroughly immediately before use because the cornflour may have settled. Return the browned tofu to the wok. Reduce the heat to medium, pour the full sauce mixture around the ingredients and toss continuously. Allow the sauce to reach a visible simmer but do not leave it stationary against the hot wok surface.

Ready when: The sauce has thickened to a glossy consistency that clings in a thin coating to the tofu, broccoli and edamame, with no watery pool remaining at the bottom of the wok.
Corrective actions
  • If: The sauce thickens before all ingredients are coated.
    Do: Add 10 ml water and toss immediately to loosen the glaze.
  • If: The sauce remains watery after 3 minutes.
    Do: Increase the heat slightly and continue tossing until visible simmering reduces the sauce to the stated glossy coating.
  • If: The sauce begins catching or scorching on the wok surface.
    Do: Remove the wok from the heat immediately, add 10 ml water and toss continuously before returning to low heat only if further thickening is required.
Step 9

Fluff the brown basmati rice

Rice
Heat source: Rice cookerSetting: Keep warm or offTime: 2–3 min

When the brown basmati rice passes its cooking endpoint, open the rice cooker and gently fluff the grains from the bottom upward. Leave the lid open for approximately 2 minutes so excess surface steam escapes.

Ready when: The rice grains are separate, tender and not visibly wet or compacted.
Corrective actions
  • If: The rice is compacted.
    Do: Lift and separate the grains gently rather than stirring vigorously, which would increase breakage.
Step 10

Finish with toasted sesame

Finishing
Time: 0.5–1 min

Remove the wok from direct heat. Add approximately two-thirds of the toasted sesame seeds and fold through the glazed stir-fry. Reserve the remaining one-third for final garnish.

Ready when: Sesame seeds are evenly distributed through the stir-fry while a visible portion remains reserved for garnish.
Corrective actions
  • If: The sauce continues thickening excessively from residual wok heat.
    Do: Transfer the stir-fry immediately to serving bowls rather than leaving it in the hot wok.
Step 11

Divide into two complete servings

Portioning
Time: 2–3 min

Divide the cooked brown basmati rice equally between 2 serving bowls. Divide the complete tofu, broccoli and edamame stir-fry equally over or alongside the rice. Scatter the reserved toasted sesame seeds equally over both servings.

Ready when: Both bowls contain approximately equal quantities of rice, tofu, broccoli, edamame, sauce and sesame garnish.
Corrective actions
  • If: One serving is visibly heavier than the other.
    Do: Use the kitchen scales to transfer components between bowls until the portions are approximately equal.
Serving

Serve immediately while the tofu edges remain crisp and the broccoli retains bite. Place the brown basmati rice as the base or to one side of each bowl, distribute the glossy miso-ginger tofu, broccoli and edamame over the rice, and finish with the reserved toasted sesame seeds.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Freezer: 1 months

Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate in sealed containers at 5°C or below for up to 2 days. For freezing, cool completely and freeze individual portions for up to 1 month; broccoli will soften somewhat after thawing. Reheat only once and ensure the tofu, rice, vegetables and sauce are steaming hot throughout before serving.

Reheating

  • Hob: place one refrigerated serving in a covered frying pan or saucepan with 15-30 ml water. Heat over medium-low heat, stirring regularly, until steaming hot throughout and the sauce has returned to a glossy consistency.
  • Air fryer plus hob: reheat tofu and vegetables in the air fryer at 170°C until thoroughly hot while reheating the rice separately on the hob with 15-30 ml water. This method better restores some tofu surface texture.
  • From frozen: thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating using one of the stated methods.
