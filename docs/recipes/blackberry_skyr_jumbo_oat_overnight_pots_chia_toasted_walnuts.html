Blackberry, Skyr & Jumbo Oat Overnight Pots with Chia and Toasted Walnuts

High-protein overnight pots combining creamy plain skyr, chewy jumbo oats and chia with a sharp-sweet crushed blackberry and lemon ripple, finished with fresh blackberries and walnuts toasted immediately before serving for aromatic crunch.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Place the as-bought blackberries in a colander or sieve. Rinse gently under cold running water without crushing them. Drain, spread on clean kitchen paper or a clean cloth and dry thoroughly. Weigh 120 g into the small bowl for the ripple and reserve 60 g intact in a covered refrigerated container for serving.

Ready when: The berries are clean and dry, with 120 g allocated to the ripple and 60 g reserved whole.

Prep 2

Wash and dry the whole lemon. Using the fine grater, remove approximately 2 g of the yellow outer zest only; stop before exposing significant white pith. Cut the lemon in half on the chopping board and squeeze 10 ml juice into the measuring jug, removing any seeds. Reserve the unused lemon for another purpose.

Ready when: Approximately 2 g fine yellow zest and exactly 10 ml lemon juice are ready for use, with no seeds or large pieces of pith.

Prep 3

Inspect the walnuts for shell fragments and discard any found. Using the chef's knife, roughly chop the walnuts into approximately 5-8 mm pieces. Do not toast yet.

Ready when: 20 g walnuts are evenly chopped into coarse pieces suitable for dry-toasting.

Prep 4

Weigh 100 g jumbo oats, 400 g skyr, 20 g chia seeds and 12 g honey. Measure 150 ml semi-skimmed milk, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon and 0.125 tsp fine salt. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until immediately before assembly.

Ready when: All ingredients are accurately measured and ready for immediate assembly.

Method

Step 1

Crush and season the blackberry ripple

Blackberry ripple
Time: 2–3 min

Place the allocated 120 g blackberries in the small bowl. Add all 12 g honey, the prepared 10 ml lemon juice and approximately 2 g lemon zest. Using the back of a spoon, press and stir the berries until approximately two-thirds of the fruit has broken down while visible small berry pieces remain. Continue stirring until the honey is completely dispersed.

Ready when: The mixture is glossy and spoonable with released purple juice, no concentrated streaks of honey and some identifiable blackberry pieces remaining.
Corrective actions
  • If: The mixture remains mostly whole and does not release enough juice to form a ripple.
    Do: Continue pressing the berries firmly against the side of the bowl in 20-second intervals until sufficient juice is released.
  • If: The mixture becomes completely smooth.
    Do: Do not process further; retain the mixture as made. The flavour remains acceptable, although the intended fruit-piece texture will be reduced.
Step 2

Combine the skyr, oats and chia base

Overnight oat base
Time: 2–3 min

Add 400 g skyr and 150 ml semi-skimmed milk to the mixing bowl and stir until uniformly loosened. Add 100 g jumbo oats, 20 g chia seeds, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon and 0.125 tsp fine salt. Fold and stir thoroughly, scraping the base and sides of the bowl, until every visible oat flake and chia seed is coated and no dry pockets remain.

Ready when: The mixture is thick but readily spoonable, homogeneous except for visible oats and chia, with no dry clumps or unmixed skyr.
Corrective actions
  • If: Dry oats or chia remain trapped at the base or sides of the bowl.
    Do: Scrape the affected area and fold repeatedly until all dry material is completely incorporated.
  • If: The mixture appears unusually loose immediately after mixing.
    Do: Proceed without adding additional oats; chia and oats will absorb liquid during refrigeration.
Step 3

Layer the overnight pots

Assembly
Time: 3–5 min

Place the first lidded container on the kitchen scales and tare. Add approximately one-quarter of the oat mixture, followed by approximately one-quarter of the blackberry ripple. Repeat once in the same container. Repeat the complete operation with the second container so that both pots receive equal quantities. Use a spoon to make one or two shallow folds through each pot, producing visible blackberry streaks without fully homogenising the colour. Fit the lids immediately.

Ready when: Both containers contain approximately equal portions with distinct purple blackberry ripples distributed through the pale oat-skyr base.
Corrective actions
  • If: One pot is visibly heavier than the other.
    Do: Use the scales to transfer small amounts between containers until the total filled weights are approximately equal.
  • If: The blackberry ripple has been fully mixed in and no streaking remains.
    Do: Do not continue mixing. Flavour will remain correct; only the intended visual contrast will be reduced.
Step 4

Refrigerate until fully hydrated

Overnight hydration
Heat source: RefrigeratorTemperature: 5°CTime: 480–720 min

Transfer the sealed containers immediately to a refrigerator operating at 5°C or below. Refrigerate undisturbed for a minimum of 8 hours and preferably overnight. Do not proceed to final service until the oats and chia have hydrated sufficiently to produce a cohesive spoonable texture.

Ready when: The jumbo oats are tender while retaining gentle chew, the chia is fully hydrated without dry seed clusters, and no free pool of milk remains at the base of either container.
Corrective actions
  • If: A small amount of free liquid remains visible after 8 hours.
    Do: Stir the pot thoroughly and refrigerate for an additional 30-60 minutes before serving.
  • If: Dry chia or oat clusters remain after refrigeration.
    Do: Break the clusters apart thoroughly with a spoon and allow a further 30 minutes of refrigerated hydration.
Step 5

Dry-toast the walnuts

Walnut garnish
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: Low-mediumTime: 3–5 min

Place the dry frying pan over a low-to-medium gas flame. Add the prepared 20 g chopped walnuts in a single layer. Toast while shaking or stirring the pan every 15-20 seconds so the pieces colour evenly.

Ready when: The walnuts smell distinctly nutty, appear slightly deeper golden-brown than when raw and are hot throughout, with no blackened edges or acrid aroma.
Corrective actions
  • If: The walnuts begin darkening rapidly or produce smoke.
    Do: Immediately remove the pan from the heat, transfer the walnuts to a cool plate and discard any visibly blackened pieces.
  • If: Little aroma or colour develops after 5 minutes.
    Do: Increase the flame slightly and continue in 30-second intervals while stirring continuously until the stated endpoint is reached.
Step 6

Check texture and finish the pots

Final service
Time: 2–3 min

Remove the overnight pots from the refrigerator. Stir each pot from the bottom upward exactly 2-3 times to redistribute any minor settling while retaining visible blackberry marbling. Divide the reserved 60 g whole blackberries equally between the two pots, approximately 30 g each. Divide the freshly toasted 20 g walnuts equally over the surface, approximately 10 g each. Serve immediately so the walnuts remain crisp.

Ready when: Each serving is creamy and spoonable with tender-chewy oats, visible blackberry streaks, fresh whole berries and warm or room-temperature crisp toasted walnuts on top.
Corrective actions
  • If: The oat mixture has become unevenly compacted during refrigeration.
    Do: Fold gently from the base upward until the texture becomes uniform; do not aggressively beat the mixture.
  • If: Condensation or berry juice has accumulated visibly on the surface.
    Do: Fold it gently into the oat mixture before adding the fresh blackberry and walnut garnish.
Serving

Serve one chilled pot per person. Keep the fresh blackberries concentrated toward the centre and scatter the toasted walnut pieces across the top so every spoonful can include contrasting creamy, juicy and crunchy elements.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

The assembled oat, skyr and blackberry mixture may be stored sealed at 5°C or below for up to 2 days. For the best texture, keep the 60 g serving blackberries and the un-toasted walnuts separate until required. Toast the walnuts immediately before serving. Do not leave the finished pots at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
