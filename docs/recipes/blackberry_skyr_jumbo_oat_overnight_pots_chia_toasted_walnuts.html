Mise En Place

Item 1
Prep Id
PREP_01

Ingredient Ids

blackberries_fresh
Instruction
Place the as-bought blackberries in a colander or sieve. Rinse gently under cold running water without crushing them. Drain, spread on clean kitchen paper or a clean cloth and dry thoroughly. Weigh 120 g into the small bowl for the ripple and reserve 60 g intact in a covered refrigerated container for serving.
Endpoint
The berries are clean and dry, with 120 g allocated to the ripple and 60 g reserved whole.
Item 2
Prep Id
PREP_02

Ingredient Ids

lemon
Instruction
Wash and dry the whole lemon. Using the fine grater, remove approximately 2 g of the yellow outer zest only; stop before exposing significant white pith. Cut the lemon in half on the chopping board and squeeze 10 ml juice into the measuring jug, removing any seeds. Reserve the unused lemon for another purpose.
Endpoint
Approximately 2 g fine yellow zest and exactly 10 ml lemon juice are ready for use, with no seeds or large pieces of pith.
Item 3

Instruction
Inspect the walnuts for shell fragments and discard any found. Using the chef's knife, roughly chop the walnuts into approximately 5-8 mm pieces. Do not toast yet.
Endpoint
20 g walnuts are evenly chopped into coarse pieces suitable for dry-toasting.

Restaurant Quality Features

A separately prepared blackberry, honey and lemon ripple gives sharper fruit definition than simply mixing whole berries into the oats.
Lemon zest and juice reinforce the natural blackberry acidity and prevent the dairy-oat base tasting flat.
Vanilla, cinnamon and a measured amount of fine salt deepen perceived sweetness without requiring excessive added sugar.
Jumbo oats are used for deliberate tender-chewy texture rather than a homogeneous porridge consistency.
Walnuts are toasted immediately before serving to maximise aromatic flavour and preserve crisp contrast against the chilled creamy base.
Fresh reserved blackberries provide a second fruit texture in addition to the crushed overnight ripple.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete serving including honey, milk, chia seeds, walnuts and all garnishes.
Plain skyr provides the majority of the protein while keeping saturated fat relatively low.
Oats, chia seeds, blackberries and walnuts provide substantial dietary fibre.
Added honey is deliberately limited because ripe blackberries, dairy and vanilla already provide perceived sweetness.
Nutrition values are estimates and will vary with the exact skyr, milk, oat and walnut products purchased.

Critical Success Factors

Use jumbo rolled oats rather than instant oats so the overnight texture remains distinct rather than pasty.
Thoroughly disperse chia seeds before refrigeration; dry clumps will not hydrate evenly.
Allow at least 8 hours of refrigerated hydration before serving.
Keep the reserved serving berries dry to prevent unnecessary surface dilution.
Do not toast the walnuts in advance if maximum crunch is required.
Remove the walnuts from the heat as soon as they become aromatic and lightly browned because their high oil content makes the transition from toasted to burnt rapid.
