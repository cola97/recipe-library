Mise En Place

Item 1
Prep Id
PREP_01

Ingredient Ids

clementines
Instruction
Wash the 2 whole clementines under cold running water and dry them thoroughly. Peel each clementine completely. Separate the fruit into individual segments. Remove loose white pith and discard it. Inspect the segments and remove any seeds encountered without excessively breaking the fruit.
Endpoint
Two peeled clementines are separated into clean, intact segments with loose pith and seeds removed.
Item 2
Prep Id
PREP_02

Ingredient Ids

pistachios_unsalted
Instruction
Inspect 32 g shelled pistachios and remove any shell fragments. Transfer to a chopping board and use the chef's knife to cut the nuts into irregular pieces approximately 4-6 mm across. Do not reduce them to crumbs.
Endpoint
The pistachios are evenly coarse-chopped, with visible pieces large enough to retain crunch.

Endpoint
All seed and seasoning components are accurately measured and immediately available.

Storage Instructions
Refrigerate the assembled pots at 5°C or below and consume within 24 hours. The snack is suitable for transport to work or on a car journey; keep chilled in an insulated lunch bag with an ice pack if it will be outside refrigeration for an extended period. For maximum crunch, the toasted pistachio and pumpkin seed mixture may instead be stored separately in a sealed dry container and added immediately before eating.

Restaurant Quality Features

Dry-toasting the pistachios and pumpkin seeds develops nuttier flavour and a crisper texture than using them directly from the packet.
A restrained maple glaze helps cinnamon and salt adhere while avoiding an excessively sweet or sticky snack.
Fine salt is deliberately included to sharpen the natural sweetness and acidity of the clementine.
Cooling the toasted topping completely before sealing reduces condensation and helps preserve crunch.
Keeping the juicy fruit and crisp topping texturally distinct provides contrast despite the recipe's simplicity.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete snack pot including clementine, pistachios, pumpkin seeds, maple syrup, cinnamon and salt.
This recipe is designed as a morning snack at approximately 200 kcal per serving.
The general 30 g protein target does not apply to morning snacks under the meal-plan rules; the approximately 6 g protein per serving is therefore compliant with the specified snack requirements.
Most of the fat is naturally occurring unsaturated fat from pistachios and pumpkin seeds.
Nutrition values are estimates and will vary with the size of the clementines and the exact supermarket products used.

Critical Success Factors

Do not over-toast the pistachios or pumpkin seeds; their relatively high oil content allows them to move rapidly from toasted to burnt.
Apply the maple syrup only after removing the toasted mixture from direct heat to reduce the risk of scorching the sugars.
Cool the nut-and-seed mixture completely before sealing the pots.
Avoid compressing or heavily mixing the clementine segments because released juice will soften the toasted topping.
For journeys substantially longer than normal commuting time, transport the pots in an insulated bag with an ice pack.
