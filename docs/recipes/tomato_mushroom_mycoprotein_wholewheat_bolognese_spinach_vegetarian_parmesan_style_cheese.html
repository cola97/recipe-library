Tomato, Mushroom & Mycoprotein Wholewheat Bolognese with Spinach and Vegetarian Parmesan-Style Cheese

Recipe info

A rich, high-protein wholewheat pasta bolognese with deeply browned mycoprotein and chestnut mushrooms, a reduced tomato sauce, wilted spinach and a savoury vegetarian Parmesan-style finish.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Peel the onion, trim the ends and dice the usable flesh into approximately 5 mm pieces. Trim the carrot and coarsely grate it. Peel and finely grate or crush the garlic. Trim the mushroom stem ends and slice the mushrooms approximately 5 mm thick.

Ready when: The onion, carrot, garlic and mushrooms are prepared for rapid sequential cooking.

Prep 2

Wash the spinach if required and dry thoroughly. Remove only unusually thick or damaged stems.

Ready when: The spinach is clean and dry enough to wilt without unnecessarily watering the sauce.

Prep 3

Finely grate the 25 g vegetarian Parmesan-style hard cheese.

Ready when: The cheese is finely grated so a relatively small amount distributes evenly over both portions.

Method

Step 1

Brown the mushrooms

Sauce
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: medium-highTime: 5–7 min

Heat the sauté pan over medium-high heat for approximately 1 minute. Add 1 tsp olive oil and the sliced mushrooms. Spread them into a broad layer and cook for approximately 5-7 minutes, stirring only every 60-90 seconds at first.

Ready when: The mushroom liquid has evaporated and the mushroom surfaces have distinct deep golden-brown patches.
Corrective actions
  • If: The mushrooms release liquid and remain grey.
    Do: Keep the pan uncovered at medium-high heat and avoid stirring until the visible liquid has evaporated.
  • If: The mushrooms darken before their released liquid evaporates.
    Do: Reduce the heat slightly and continue until the pan is dry.
Step 2

Cook the aromatics and brown the mycoprotein

Sauce
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: medium-highTime: 7–9 min

Add the remaining 1 tsp olive oil, diced onion and grated carrot to the browned mushrooms. Cook for approximately 3-4 minutes until the onion begins to soften. Add the 300 g mycoprotein mince and 1/2 tsp black pepper, increase the heat to medium-high and cook for another 4-5 minutes, stirring only often enough to prevent catching.

Ready when: The onion is softened, the carrot has collapsed into the mixture and the mycoprotein has developed visible browned areas rather than remaining uniformly pale.
Corrective actions
  • If: The mixture releases enough liquid to prevent browning.
    Do: Keep the pan uncovered and continue at medium-high heat until the visible moisture evaporates before proceeding.
  • If: The onion or carrot begins catching before the mycoprotein has browned.
    Do: Reduce the heat slightly and stir more frequently.
Step 3

Cook the tomato purée and aromatics

Sauce
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: mediumTime: 1 min

Reduce the heat to medium. Add the garlic, 2 tbsp tomato purée and 1 tsp dried oregano. Stir continuously for approximately 1 minute, scraping the tomato purée across the pan base so it darkens slightly.

Ready when: The garlic and oregano smell aromatic and the tomato purée has darkened from bright red towards brick red without scorching.
Corrective actions
  • If: The garlic or tomato purée begins catching or smelling bitter.
    Do: Immediately add the chopped tomatoes and stir thoroughly.
Step 4

Reduce the bolognese sauce

Sauce
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: mediumTime: 8–10 min

Add the 400 g chopped tomatoes and 1/4 tsp fine salt. Bring to a lively simmer, then cook uncovered over medium heat for approximately 8-10 minutes, stirring every 2-3 minutes.

Ready when: The sauce is thick and glossy, no watery tomato liquid separates around the edges, and a spatula drawn across the pan base leaves a trail visible for approximately 2 seconds.
Corrective actions
  • If: The sauce begins sticking before reaching the reduction endpoint.
    Do: Reduce the heat and add 1 tbsp of the reserved pasta water once available.
  • If: The sauce remains visibly watery after 10 minutes.
    Do: Increase the heat slightly and continue uncovered until it passes the stated spatula-trail test.
Step 5

Cook the wholewheat spaghetti

Pasta
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: medium-highTime: 8–10 min

While the tomato sauce reduces, boil approximately 2 L water in the kettle and transfer it to the saucepan. Return to a vigorous boil, add 1/4 tsp fine salt and the 130 g wholewheat spaghetti. Cook according to the lower end of the package timing, normally approximately 8-10 minutes, stirring well after the pasta first enters the water.

Ready when: The spaghetti is tender through the centre but retains a slight bite and is not soft or swollen.
Corrective actions
  • If: The spaghetti reaches the correct texture before the stated package time.
    Do: Proceed immediately to reserving cooking water and draining rather than continuing to cook by the clock.
Step 6

Reserve pasta water and drain

Pasta
Time: 1–2 min

Before draining, scoop approximately 100 ml pasta cooking water into the measuring jug. Drain the spaghetti thoroughly in the colander. Do not rinse it.

Ready when: The spaghetti is well drained but retains its surface starch, and approximately 100 ml hot cooking water is reserved for sauce adjustment.
Step 7

Wilt the spinach and balance the sauce

Sauce
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: mediumTime: 1–2 min

Add the spinach to the reduced sauce in 2 additions, folding each portion through until wilted. Cook for approximately 1-2 minutes, then remove the pan from the heat and stir through 2 tsp red wine vinegar.

Ready when: The spinach is fully wilted, the sauce remains thick and glossy, and the vinegar gives a clean acidic lift without making the sauce taste sharply vinegary.
Corrective actions
  • If: The spinach releases enough water to loosen the sauce.
    Do: Return the pan to medium-high heat for 1-2 minutes and reduce until the original thick consistency returns.
Step 8

Coat the spaghetti in the bolognese

Finishing
Heat source: gas_hobSetting: lowTime: 1–2 min

Add the drained spaghetti to the bolognese sauce and toss thoroughly over low heat for approximately 1 minute. Add reserved pasta water 1 tbsp at a time only if required to make the sauce cling evenly to the spaghetti.

Ready when: Every strand of spaghetti is coated in the thick tomato-mushroom sauce, with no watery liquid at the bottom of the pan and no dry clumps of pasta.
Corrective actions
  • If: The sauce is too thick to coat the spaghetti evenly.
    Do: Add 1 tbsp reserved pasta water, toss thoroughly and repeat only if required.
  • If: The sauce becomes loose after adding pasta water.
    Do: Continue tossing over medium heat for approximately 1 minute until the excess moisture evaporates.
Serving

Divide the wholewheat spaghetti and bolognese evenly between two warm bowls. Scatter half of the finely grated vegetarian Parmesan-style cheese over each serving and serve immediately while the sauce is glossy and the cheese is freshly melting.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Freezer: 1 months

Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate in a sealed container for up to 2 days. The bolognese sauce without pasta may be frozen for up to 1 month. For the best texture, freeze sauce separately and cook fresh pasta after defrosting.

Reheating

  • Hob: place leftover pasta and sauce in a covered sauté pan over low-medium heat with 1-2 tbsp water and heat, stirring regularly, until piping hot throughout.
  • Oven: place in a covered ovenproof dish with 1-2 tbsp water and heat at approximately 180°C until piping hot throughout.
  • For frozen sauce, defrost safely in the refrigerator, then reheat on the hob until piping hot before combining with freshly cooked pasta.
