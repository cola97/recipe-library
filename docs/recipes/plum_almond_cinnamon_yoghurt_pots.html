Plum, Almond & Cinnamon Yoghurt Pots

Recipe info

Creamy Greek yoghurt topped with ripe cinnamon-dressed plums and crunchy almonds, with just enough honey to round out the fruit's acidity while keeping the snack fresh, portable and simple.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Halve the washed plums around the stones, twist the halves apart and discard the stones. Cut the plum flesh into approximately 12 mm pieces, retaining the edible skins.

Ready when: The plums are evenly diced into bite-sized pieces without being crushed or releasing excessive juice.

Prep 2

Weigh 24 g almonds and roughly chop them into coarse pieces approximately 4-6 mm across.

Ready when: The almonds are coarse enough to provide distinct crunch rather than fine crumbs.

Method

Step 1

Dress the plums

Fruit
Time: 1–2 min

Place the diced plums in the small bowl. Add 1 tsp runny honey and 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon, then fold gently until the fruit is evenly coated without crushing the pieces.

Ready when: The plum pieces are lightly glossy and evenly cinnamon-coated, with their shape still intact and no large pool of liquid in the bowl.
Corrective actions
  • If: The plums are extremely ripe and begin breaking down during mixing.
    Do: Stop mixing as soon as the cinnamon and honey are distributed and spoon the fruit directly into the pots.
Step 2

Build the yoghurt pots

Assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Divide the 300 g Greek yoghurt evenly between the two snack pots, giving approximately 150 g per serving. Spoon half of the cinnamon plum mixture over each portion of yoghurt. Do not stir the fruit fully through the yoghurt; keeping the layers distinct preserves a fresher texture.

Ready when: Each pot contains an even portion of thick yoghurt topped with distinct pieces of cinnamon-dressed plum.
Corrective actions
  • If: The dressed plums have released substantial juice.
    Do: Use a slotted spoon or leave excess juice behind in the mixing bowl so the yoghurt does not become watery during transport.
Step 3

Pack the almonds separately

Assembly
Time: 1–2 min

Divide the chopped almonds evenly between the two dry compartments or separate small containers, giving approximately half to each serving. Seal the yoghurt pots and almond portions securely and refrigerate until required.

Ready when: Both yoghurt pots are sealed and the almonds remain completely separate from the moist fruit and yoghurt.
Corrective actions
  • If: The snack pots do not have separate dry compartments.
    Do: Carry the almonds in two small separate reusable containers and add them immediately before eating.
Serving

At the time of eating, scatter the chopped almonds over the plum and yoghurt. Eat immediately after adding the almonds so their crunch contrasts with the creamy yoghurt and juicy fruit.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 1 days

Refrigerate the assembled yoghurt and plum pots promptly and use within 24 hours for the best fruit texture. Keep the almonds completely separate until eating. For transport to work or on a car journey, keep the yoghurt pots chilled in an insulated bag with an ice pack and do not leave them at room temperature for prolonged periods.
