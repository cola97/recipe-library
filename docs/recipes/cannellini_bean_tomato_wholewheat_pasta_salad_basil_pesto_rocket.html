Cannellini Bean, Tomato & Wholewheat Pasta Salad with Basil Pesto and Rocket

A portable high-protein wholewheat pasta salad with creamy cannellini beans, ripe tomatoes and peppery rocket, bound with a bright basil-pesto dressing enriched with blended cottage cheese and lemon for additional protein and freshness without excessive oil.

Mise en place

Prep 1

Open the cannellini bean tin. Pour the contents into a colander and discard the canning liquid. Rinse the beans thoroughly under cold running water while gently turning them with clean fingers. Shake the colander and leave the beans to drain for at least 2 minutes.

Ready when: Approximately 240 g cannellini beans are clean, intact and free from visible canning liquid.

Prep 2

Wash the cherry tomatoes and rocket if required by the packaging instructions, then dry thoroughly. Remove any tomato stems and halve each tomato through the stem axis. Remove damaged or slimy rocket leaves and discard them.

Ready when: The tomatoes are clean, dry and evenly halved, and the rocket is clean, dry and free from damaged leaves.

Prep 3

Wash and dry the lemon. Finely grate approximately 2 g yellow zest without removing significant white pith. Cut the lemon and squeeze 20 ml juice, removing all seeds. Separate 1 small garlic clove, peel it, trim the root end and grate finely. Finely grate 20 g vegetarian Parmesan-style cheese.

Ready when: Lemon zest, lemon juice, garlic and hard cheese are finely prepared and ready for blending.

Prep 4

Weigh 65 g dry wholewheat pasta, 220 g low-fat cottage cheese, 12 g basil pesto and 4 g extra-virgin olive oil. Measure 0.25 tsp initial fine salt, 0.125 tsp reserved fine salt, 0.25 tsp black pepper and 20 ml cold water.

Ready when: All remaining ingredients are accurately measured and immediately available.

Method

Step 1

Cook the wholewheat pasta

Pasta
Heat source: Gas hobSetting: High, then medium-highTemperature: 100°CTime: 8–12 min

Fill the saucepan with sufficient water to allow the 65 g pasta to move freely and bring to a full boil over high gas heat. Add approximately 0.125 tsp of the measured initial salt, then add the wholewheat pasta. Stir immediately to prevent sticking and maintain a steady active boil.

Ready when: The pasta is fully cooked but retains a slight firm bite at the centre and shows no chalky raw core.
Corrective actions
  • If: The water threatens to boil over.
    Do: Reduce the gas flame while maintaining an active boil.
  • If: The pasta remains noticeably hard at the maximum stated time.
    Do: Continue cooking in 1-minute intervals and reassess after each interval.
Step 2

Blend the basil-pesto dressing

Dressing
Time: 1–3 min

Add 220 g cottage cheese, 12 g basil pesto, 20 g grated vegetarian Parmesan-style cheese, 20 ml lemon juice, approximately 2 g lemon zest, the finely grated garlic, 4 g extra-virgin olive oil, approximately 0.125 tsp of the initial salt and 0.25 tsp black pepper to the blender. Blend until smooth. If the mixture does not circulate freely or remains thicker than a spoonable dressing, add the measured cold water in 5 ml increments and blend after each addition.

Ready when: The dressing is pale green, smooth, cohesive and thick enough to coat pasta and beans without running freely from a spoon.
Corrective actions
  • If: The blender blades spin without circulating the mixture.
    Do: Stop the blender, scrape down the sides and add 5 ml water before restarting.
  • If: The dressing becomes thinner than intended.
    Do: Stop adding water immediately and refrigerate the dressing while the pasta cools; do not add unlisted thickening ingredients.
Step 3

Drain and cool the pasta

Pasta
Time: 5–8 min

Drain the cooked pasta immediately through the colander. Shake firmly to remove excess water. Transfer the pasta to the large mixing bowl and spread it into a thin layer. Leave uncovered, turning once or twice with the spatula so steam escapes evenly.

Ready when: The pasta is no longer visibly steaming, feels warm rather than hot and has no standing water in the bowl.
Corrective actions
  • If: The pasta begins sticking into compact clumps while cooling.
    Do: Separate the pieces gently with the spatula; do not add additional oil because the dressing will provide lubrication.
  • If: Water accumulates at the base of the bowl.
    Do: Return the pasta to the colander briefly, drain again and then resume cooling.
Step 4

Coat the pasta and beans

Base assembly
Time: 2–3 min

Add the drained cannellini beans to the cooled pasta. Add all of the prepared basil-pesto dressing. Fold gently from the bottom upward until the pasta and beans are evenly coated. Avoid vigorous stirring, which can crush the beans.

Ready when: The pasta and beans are uniformly coated with a thin creamy green dressing, with the cannellini beans remaining predominantly intact.
Corrective actions
  • If: The dressing remains concentrated in one area.
    Do: Continue folding from multiple directions until the coating is evenly distributed.
  • If: The mixture appears excessively thick after coating.
    Do: Add any remaining measured water in 5 ml increments and fold gently until the dressing coats rather than clumps.
Step 5

Add the tomatoes and rocket

Vegetable assembly
Time: 1–2 min

Add the 180 g halved cherry tomatoes and 50 g dry rocket to the dressed pasta and beans. Fold slowly and only until the vegetables are distributed throughout the salad.

Ready when: Tomatoes and rocket are evenly distributed, the tomato halves remain structurally intact and the rocket is coated lightly rather than crushed or heavily wilted.
Corrective actions
  • If: The rocket begins compressing or bruising during mixing.
    Do: Stop mixing immediately and complete distribution during portioning rather than continuing to fold.
Step 6

Check final seasoning

Seasoning
Time: 1–2 min

Taste one representative mouthful containing pasta, bean, tomato and dressing. If the flavour is already clearly seasoned, acidic and savoury, do not add the reserved salt. If noticeably under-seasoned, add up to the reserved 0.125 tsp fine salt in 0.0625 tsp increments, folding gently after each addition.

Ready when: The salad tastes balanced, with identifiable basil, lemon, tomato sweetness and savoury cheese, without excessive saltiness or oiliness.
Corrective actions
  • If: The salad is adequately seasoned before the reserved salt is used.
    Do: Do not add the reserved salt.
Step 7

Divide and chill the portable lunches

Packing
Heat source: RefrigeratorTemperature: 5°CTime: 10 min minimum

Divide the complete salad equally between 2 clean lunch containers using the kitchen scales. Ensure each portion contains approximately equal quantities of pasta, beans, tomatoes, rocket and dressing. Secure the lids and refrigerate immediately at 5°C or below.

Ready when: Both containers contain approximately equal complete servings and are fully chilled before transport.
Corrective actions
  • If: One portion contains visibly fewer beans or tomatoes than the other.
    Do: Redistribute individual components between containers before sealing.
  • If: The salad is still noticeably warm when the containers are filled.
    Do: Leave the containers open in the refrigerator until the salad is chilled, then fit the lids securely.
Serving

Serve one chilled or cool-room-temperature portion per person. Before eating, turn the salad gently once or twice with a fork to redistribute any dressing that has settled, taking care not to mash the cannellini beans.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerator: 2 days

Store at 5°C or below and consume within 2 days. For transport to work or on a car journey, keep the lunch chilled in an insulated bag with an ice pack when continuous refrigeration is unavailable. The salad is intended to be eaten chilled or cool rather than reheated. Rocket will soften gradually during storage, so texture is best within 24 hours.
