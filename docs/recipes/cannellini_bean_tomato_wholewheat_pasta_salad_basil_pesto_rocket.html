Storage Instructions
Store at 5°C or below and consume within 2 days. For transport to work or on a car journey, keep the lunch chilled in an insulated bag with an ice pack when continuous refrigeration is unavailable. The salad is intended to be eaten chilled or cool rather than reheated. Rocket will soften gradually during storage, so texture is best within 24 hours.

Restaurant Quality Features

Cannellini beans provide creamy texture and substantial protein while remaining intact through gentle folding.
Cottage cheese is blended into the basil pesto dressing to create a smooth, protein-rich coating without requiring a large quantity of oil.
Vegetarian Parmesan-style hard cheese reinforces savoury depth and provides additional protein.
Fresh lemon juice and zest balance the richness of pesto and cheese and keep the cold salad tasting bright.
Garlic is grated directly into the dressing so its flavour disperses evenly without harsh chunks.
Wholewheat pasta is cooked only until slightly firm so it retains structure after chilling and dressing.
Tomatoes and rocket are added after the pasta has cooled, preventing unnecessary wilting and preserving textural contrast.
The pasta is cooled without oil so the dressing adheres directly to the grain surface rather than sliding off.

Nutrition Notes

Estimated nutrition represents one complete lunch serving including pasta, cannellini beans, tomatoes, rocket, cottage cheese, pesto, vegetarian hard cheese, olive oil and all dressing components.
The recipe is designed around the approximately 400 kcal lunch target.
Approximately 31 g protein per serving comes from cannellini beans, cottage cheese, wholewheat pasta and vegetarian hard cheese.
Cannellini beans, wholewheat pasta, tomatoes and rocket provide substantial dietary fibre and plant diversity.
Pesto and olive oil are deliberately used in restrained quantities because their flavour is concentrated and larger amounts would substantially increase calories without being necessary for coating.
Nutrition values are estimates and will vary with the exact pasta, cottage cheese, pesto, beans and cheese selected.
The recipe follows the supplied structured-recipe and lunch requirements.

Critical Success Factors

Do not overcook the wholewheat pasta; it should retain slight bite before cooling.
Drain both the pasta and cannellini beans thoroughly because excess water will dilute the dressing.
Blend the cottage-cheese pesto dressing until smooth before combining it with the salad.
Cool the pasta before adding rocket so the leaves remain fresh rather than heat-wilted.
Fold rather than vigorously stir once cannellini beans and tomatoes are present to preserve their structure.
Taste before using the reserved salt because pesto and hard cheese can vary substantially in salt content.
Keep the completed salad chilled during storage and transport.
